Best Surge Protectors in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 22, 2026 9 min read

A surge protector is one of the most ignored but critical purchases for anyone with electronics worth protecting. A $15 strip from a gas station with no surge rating offers zero protection. Quality surge protectors rated 1,000+ joules with UL certification and connected equipment warranties genuinely protect your TV, computer, and gaming console.

We evaluated joule rating, outlet configuration, USB-A and USB-C port inclusion, cord length, indicator light reliability, and connected equipment warranty to find the best surge protectors.

What to Look For

  • Joule rating: Higher joule ratings absorb more surge energy before the protector is overwhelmed. 1,000-2,000 joules covers home electronics. Sensitive equipment like home theaters and computers benefits from 3,000+ joules. Avoid strips that do not list a joule rating.
  • UL 1449 certification: UL 1449 is the safety standard for surge protectors. Any strip without this certification has not been independently tested for surge suppression performance. Do not buy uncertified surge protectors for anything important.
  • Connected equipment warranty: Premium surge protectors include a $50,000-500,000 warranty on connected equipment if lightning or a power surge damages gear while plugged in. Read the terms — warranties often require proper installation and registration.
  • Outlet spacing: Wide-spaced outlets accommodate wall adapters without blocking adjacent outlets. Rotating or pivoting outlets handle bulky adapters in multiple configurations.
  • USB-C Power Delivery: USB-C PD ports on a surge protector can charge a laptop, phone, or tablet at full speed without a separate wall charger. 45-65W USB-C PD is the useful minimum for laptop charging.
