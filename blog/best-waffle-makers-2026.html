Best Waffle Makers in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 26, 2026 8 min read

A great waffle maker produces a crispy exterior and fluffy interior without requiring babysitting. The difference between a $15 waffle iron and a $100 one is temperature consistency — cheap units cycle on and off wildly, producing pale centers or burnt edges. In 2026 the best models maintain temperature with precision thermostats and signal exactly when to pour and when the waffle is ready.

We evaluated temperature control, plate quality, indicator reliability, cleanup ease, and value across Belgian and classic American waffle styles.

What to Look For

  • Belgian vs. classic: Belgian waffles have deeper pockets (for syrup and toppings) and are thicker. Classic American waffles are thinner and crisper. Both are good — choose based on preference and recipe.
  • Indicator accuracy: A reliable indicator light or audible signal that tells you when the waffle is actually done is the most important feature. Units without reliable indicators require constant attention.
  • Flip mechanism: Rotating waffle makers flip 180° mid-cook to distribute batter evenly into deep pockets — this is how restaurants get uniformly deep Belgian waffles. Non-rotating makes thinner, less even results.
  • Plate coating: PTFE nonstick is standard — avoid rough or thin coatings that food sticks to. Cleanup should require only a damp wipe.
  • Drip moat: Overflowing batter is a mess. A built-in drip channel around the plates catches overflow — essential if you like filling the plates generously.
