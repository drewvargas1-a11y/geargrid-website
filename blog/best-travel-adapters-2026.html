Best Travel Adapters of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 12, 2026 8 min read

A quality travel adapter is the difference between a connected trip and an electronics-dead nightmare. Modern universal adapters cover over 200 countries, include USB-C Power Delivery for fast charging, and protect your devices from voltage spikes.

These are the best travel adapters of 2026 — combining universal plug compatibility, fast USB-C charging, and surge protection in compact, travel-ready designs.

What to Look For

  • Plug Type Coverage: Quality universal adapters cover Types A, B, C, G, I (covers USA, UK, EU, Australia, and most of Asia). Confirm specific country coverage for your destination.
  • USB-C Power Delivery: USB-C PD allows charging laptops, tablets, and modern smartphones at full speed. Look for 30W+ USB-C PD if you're charging a laptop.
  • Surge Protection: Built-in surge protection guards electronics from voltage spikes common in developing countries. Non-negotiable for protecting laptops and cameras.
  • Input Voltage Range: Most modern electronics (laptops, phones) accept 100–240V automatically. The adapter only converts the plug shape, not voltage — check your device specs.
  • Number of Ports: More simultaneous USB ports (ideally 3+) reduce cables and adapters needed. A combination of USB-A and USB-C covers all device types.
