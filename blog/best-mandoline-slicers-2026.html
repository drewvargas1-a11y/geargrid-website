Best Mandoline Slicers in 2026: Fast, Uniform Slices Every Time

By GearGrid Staff July 23, 2026 8 min read

A mandoline slicer cuts vegetables 10x faster than a knife for tasks that require uniform thickness — gratins, cucumber salads, potato chips, thin-sliced fennel, and coleslaw. The uniformity matters: uneven slices cook at different rates, which means some pieces are burnt while others are undercooked. A mandoline eliminates that problem entirely.

Here are the best mandoline slicers of 2026 — from simple adjustable-thickness slicers for everyday prep to multi-blade models for julienne and waffle cuts — ranked by cut quality, safety, and how easy they are to actually use without injury.

What to Look For

  • Safety — the most important spec: Mandoline blades are among the sharpest cutting edges in any kitchen. A proper hand guard (food holder that grips the vegetable) is non-negotiable. Never use a mandoline without the guard, regardless of how good you think your knife skills are.
  • Blade adjustment: Dial or lever thickness adjustment lets you go from paper-thin (1mm) for chips to 8mm+ for thick-sliced cucumbers. Stepless (infinite adjustment) is more versatile than click-step adjustments.
  • Blade types: Straight blade (slicing), julienne blade (thin matchsticks), French fry blade (thick matchsticks), waffle blade (crinkle cuts). Most home cooks need only straight + julienne.
  • Material: Japanese stainless steel blades (ceramic on premium models) hold their edge longer. Plastic frames are lightweight; stainless or ceramic frames are more durable. The frame rigidity determines whether the mandoline rocks during cutting.
  • Cleanup: Mandoline cleanup is a cut hazard — use the brush or sponge held in one hand and handle only the frame, never run your fingers along the blade. Dishwasher-safe models are safer to clean.
