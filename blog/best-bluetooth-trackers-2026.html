Best Bluetooth Trackers in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 8, 2026 9 min read

Bluetooth trackers are the simplest technology you can add to your everyday carry. Attach one to your keys, slip one in your wallet, throw one in your bag, and you will never spend ten minutes searching for something before leaving the house again. The category matured significantly when Apple launched AirTag in 2021, and competitors have matched or exceeded it in several areas.

We evaluated tracking range, network size, battery life, and privacy protections to find the best Bluetooth trackers in 2026.

What to Look For

  • Network size: The Find My network (AirTag) is the largest crowdsourced location network in the world. Tile and others use their own networks, which are smaller but cross-platform.
  • Platform: AirTag is iPhone-only for precision finding. Tile and Chipolo work on iOS and Android equally well.
  • Precision Finding: AirTag uses Ultra Wideband for centimeter-accurate directional arrows on iPhone 11 and newer. Other trackers use Bluetooth RSSI (less precise).
  • Battery: Most trackers use replaceable CR2032 batteries lasting 12 months. Some use proprietary rechargeable batteries — check replacement cost.
  • Anti-stalking: Apple, Tile, and Samsung all now include anti-stalking alerts that notify Android or iPhone users when an unknown tracker travels with them.
