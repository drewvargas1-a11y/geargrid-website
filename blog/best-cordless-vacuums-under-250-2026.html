Best Cordless Vacuums Under $250 in 2026

GearGrid Staff June 3, 2026 8 min read

Cord vacuums are dying. Cordless stick vacuums have caught up in suction power, and the freedom to vacuum from room to room without unplugging and replanting has made them the default choice for most households. The best models in 2026 run 45–60 minutes on a charge, have self-cleaning brushrolls that don't tangle on pet hair, and weigh under 6 lbs for easy overhead and stair use.

We ranked four cordless stick vacuums under $250 on suction performance, battery life, dustbin capacity, weight, and ease of maintenance. These picks cover hardwood floors, carpets, and pet hair at every budget from $80 to $200.

What to Look For

Brushroll Type

Traditional bristle brushrolls wrap with hair over time. Self-cleaning or tangle-free brushrolls — like those in the Shark IZ462H and Bissell CleanView — actively remove hair during operation. If you have pets or long hair, pay the premium for a tangle-free design. It saves significant maintenance time over the vacuum's lifetime.

Runtime vs Suction

Higher suction modes drain batteries faster. Most cordless vacuums quote their longest runtime at the lowest suction setting. On boost/max mode, expect 15–20 minutes. For a 2-bedroom apartment, 40 minutes at standard suction is plenty. For a larger home or deep carpet cleaning, prioritize a model with a removable/swappable battery.
