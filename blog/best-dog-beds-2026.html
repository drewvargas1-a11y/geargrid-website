Best Dog Beds in 2026: Every Size and Sleep Style Ranked

By GearGrid Staff July 19, 2026 8 min read

Dogs sleep 12–14 hours a day, and the surface they sleep on directly affects joint health, hip comfort, and sleep quality — especially in older dogs with arthritis. A quality dog bed isn't a luxury; it's basic maintenance for a dog's long-term mobility.

Here are the best dog beds of 2026 across every sleep style: orthopedic foam for large breeds and seniors, calming donut beds for anxious dogs, and washable budget options for puppy owners.

What to Look For

  • Orthopedic foam: Memory foam and egg-crate foam beds conform to body contours and distribute weight evenly. Essential for large breeds, senior dogs, and any dog with hip dysplasia or arthritis.
  • Size matching: Measure your dog from nose to tail when fully stretched and add 6–12 inches. Many owners buy too small — a dog should be able to fully extend without hanging off the edge.
  • Washable covers: Waterproof liners + removable machine-washable covers are non-negotiable for puppies and senior dogs. Verify cover zippered removal, not just hand-wash.
  • Fill density: Cheap foam compresses to nothing within months. Look for CertiPUR-US certified foam with density ratings — higher density (4+ lbs/cu ft) holds its shape longer.
  • Calming vs. flat: Bolster and donut beds with raised edges help anxious dogs who like to curl. Flat orthopedic beds work better for sprawlers and dogs with mobility issues who need to step over an edge.
