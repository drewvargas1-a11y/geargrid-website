Best Portable Chargers & Power Banks in 2026

GearGrid Staff June 3, 2026 7 min read

A dead phone in the middle of a travel day, a long hike, or a work session away from a desk is one of the most avoidable inconveniences in modern life. A good portable charger eliminates it — and in 2026, the best ones do it without weighing down your bag, without taking all night to recharge themselves, and often with enough capacity to charge a phone three or four times on a single charge of the bank.

We evaluated power banks by capacity (mAh), output wattage (how fast they charge your phone), size and weight, input speed (how quickly they recharge from a wall adapter), and price. These four picks cover every need from slip-it-in-your-pocket daily carry at $22 to high-capacity travel banks at $80.

The built-in display shows remaining battery percentage and current wattage flowing through each port in real time. Anker's ActiveShield 2.0 monitors temperature 3 million times per day to prevent overheating. At 1.4 lbs it's a two-hand-carry item, not a pocket bank — this belongs in a backpack for long travel days, camping trips, or laptop charging on the go. Recharges itself in about 2 hours via the 140W input. The benchmark for high-capacity power banks.

What to Look For

Capacity: How Many Charges Do You Need?

A typical smartphone battery holds 4,000–5,000mAh. A 10,000mAh power bank delivers roughly 2 full charges accounting for conversion efficiency. A 20,000mAh bank delivers about 4 charges. For a day trip, 10K is enough. For international travel or multiple days off-grid, go 20K or above. For charging laptops, you need at least 18,000mAh and 65W+ output.

Output Wattage: How Fast Does It Charge?

The higher the output wattage, the faster your phone charges. Standard USB-A outputs 5–12W. USB-C Power Delivery outputs 18W–140W depending on the bank. Modern iPhones charge fastest at 20W+; recent Android flagships support 25–45W or more. Check your phone's fast-charge spec and match a power bank that can meet it.
