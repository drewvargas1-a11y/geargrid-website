Best Pressure Washers Under $300 in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 18, 2026 9 min read

A pressure washer cuts through grime on driveways, decks, siding, and outdoor furniture in a fraction of the time of scrubbing. The best models under $300 produce enough PSI and GPM to handle nearly any residential cleaning task without the cost or maintenance of a gas unit.

We evaluated pressure output, flow rate, motor durability, hose quality, and included accessories to find the best pressure washers under $300.

What to Look For

  • PSI and GPM: PSI (pressure) determines cutting power; GPM (gallons per minute) determines rinsing speed. Cleaning Units (CU = PSI x GPM) is the best single metric. For residential use, 1,600-2,400 CU is sufficient. Heavy concrete staining needs 3,000+ CU.
  • Electric vs. gas: Electric pressure washers are quieter, need no oil or fuel, and start with a button press. Gas models produce more pressure (3,000+ PSI) and work without an outlet but require fuel, spark plugs, and oil maintenance.
  • Hose length: 25 feet is the minimum useful hose length. 35-50 foot hoses let you reach the roof, second-story siding, and vehicles without moving the unit. Kink-resistant materials matter after the first season.
  • Nozzle variety: A set of quick-connect nozzles (0°, 15°, 25°, 40°, and soap) covers every application from blasting grime to applying detergent at low pressure. Avoid units with only 1-2 nozzles.
  • Motor type: Universal motors (brushed) are cheaper but wear faster — expect 100-200 hours of use. Induction motors (brushless) last longer and run quieter but cost more.
