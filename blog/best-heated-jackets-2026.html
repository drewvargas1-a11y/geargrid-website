Best Heated Jackets of 2026

By GearGrid Staff October 8, 2026 3 min read

A heated jacket puts thin carbon-fiber heating panels across your chest and back and powers them from a rechargeable battery pack in a pocket. The result is warmth you can turn up or down with a button — far more adjustable than simply adding another layer.

They're especially useful for activities where you stand still in the cold: watching games, walking the dog, working outdoors, or waiting for a train. We compared battery capacity, heat settings, fit, and how practical each jacket is as an everyday coat.

What to Look For

  • Battery capacity: Bigger batteries (10,000mAh and up) generally mean longer run time; expect the most hours on the low setting and far fewer on high.
  • Heat zones: More panels spread warmth across your core; look for at least chest and back zones.
  • USB power bank: Most packs double as phone chargers — handy, but it drains the heat time.
  • Detachable hood: A removable hood makes the jacket work both as a winter coat and a lighter layer.
  • Washability: Check that the battery and connector can be removed so the shell can be washed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a heated jacket battery last?

It depends on the battery size and setting. Packs around 10,000mAh typically run for several hours on low and much less on high; check each listing for its specific estimates.

Can you wash a heated jacket?

Most can be hand or machine washed on a gentle cycle once the battery is removed and connectors are tucked away. Always follow the care label.

Can you wear a heated jacket in the rain?

Many shells are water-resistant and the low-voltage system is designed for outdoor use, but they aren't meant to be submerged. Keep the battery pocket closed in wet weather.

Are heated jackets allowed on planes?

The jacket itself is fine, but lithium battery packs generally must go in carry-on luggage, not checked bags. Check your airline's current rules.
