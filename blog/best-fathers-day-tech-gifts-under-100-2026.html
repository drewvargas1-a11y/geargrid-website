Best Father's Day Tech Gifts Under $100 in 2026

GearGrid Staff May 19, 2026 6 min read

Father's Day is June 21st and tech gifts are the easiest win — dads actually use them, they don't collect dust, and you can find genuinely great options without blowing $200+. The challenge is skipping the generic stuff that ends up in a drawer and finding gear that fits how he actually spends his time.

Every pick on this list is under $100, ships with Prime, and is something a real person would be excited to unwrap. No filler.

1. Apple AirTag 4-Pack — Best for the Dad Who Loses Everything

At $99 for a 4-pack, AirTags are one of the most universally useful gifts you can buy. Keys, wallet, TV remote, luggage — attach one to anything and find it instantly from an iPhone. The precision finding feature (on iPhone 11 and newer) uses the U1 chip to guide you directly to the item with an arrow and distance indicator. Setup takes 30 seconds. Battery lasts about a year and uses a standard CR2032 coin battery.

If your dad has an iPhone, this is a near-guaranteed hit. It solves a real daily annoyance without requiring any tech savvy to use. Buy a pack of 4 keychain holders alongside it to make it a complete gift.

2. Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Max — Best for the TV Dad

The Fire TV Stick 4K Max at around $60 turns any TV with an HDMI port into a smart TV. If your dad has an older TV in the garage, bedroom, or man cave that isn't smart, this fixes it instantly. Streams Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, Disney+, and everything else in 4K HDR. The Wi-Fi 6E support makes streaming faster and more reliable than previous generations. Alexa voice remote is included so he can search without typing.

Setup takes under 10 minutes and requires nothing but a Wi-Fi password. This is the kind of gift that gets used every single day.

3. Anker Soundcore Liberty 4 NC — Best Earbuds

We covered these in our earbuds roundup and they're the best wireless earbuds under $100 — period. At around $80, the Liberty 4 NC brings genuine active noise cancellation, LDAC high-res audio for Android users, and 28 hours of total battery. Whether your dad commutes, works from home, mows the lawn, or travels — these will get used constantly.

The sound quality is well above what you'd expect at this price, and the ANC is legitimately good at blocking out background noise. This is the gift that makes someone think "why did I wait so long to get these."

4. Amazon Echo Dot (5th Gen) — Best Smart Home Starter

At just $50, the Echo Dot 5th Gen is the easiest entry point into smart home for a dad who doesn't have any. The improved speaker sounds noticeably better than previous Dots — good enough for the kitchen, office, or workshop. Alexa handles music, timers, weather, smart home control, and sports scores hands-free. If he's grilling in the backyard and needs a timer, he just says it.

The 5th Gen added a built-in temperature sensor and motion detection, which can trigger automations — lights that turn on when he enters the garage, for example. A genuinely useful gift that doesn't feel gimmicky.

🏠 Best Smart Home

Amazon Echo Dot (5th Gen)

Alexa built-in · Improved speaker · Temperature sensor · Motion detection

5. Tile Mate (4-Pack) — Best Android Alternative to AirTags

If your dad is on Android, Tile Mate is the AirTag equivalent. A 4-pack runs about $70 and works the same way — attach to keys, wallet, luggage, anything — and locate it from the Tile app. The Tile network works across all Tile users, so even if the item is out of Bluetooth range, other Tile users who pass by it anonymously update its location. Works with both Android and iPhone, making it more versatile than AirTags for households with mixed devices.
