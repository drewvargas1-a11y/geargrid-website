Best Noise-Canceling Headphones Under $150 in 2026

GearGrid Staff May 30, 2026 9 min read

Active noise cancellation used to be a $300-plus premium feature dominated by Sony and Bose. In 2026, you can get genuinely excellent ANC performance for under $150 — the technology has democratized to the point where budget-friendly headphones from Soundcore, JBL, EarFun, and even Sony's own mid-range lineup deliver noise cancellation that was premium-tier just a few years ago.

We evaluated four of the best noise-canceling headphones under $150 based on ANC performance, sound quality, comfort for extended wear, battery life, and call quality. These picks range from $60 to $130 and cover every listening priority from commuting to studio-style monitoring.

Our Top Picks

#1 — Sony WH-CH720N: Best Overall

The Sony WH-CH720N at around $100 is the best noise-canceling headphone under $150 for most people. It brings Sony's industry-leading processor — the QN1e chip, the same core technology in the premium WH-1000XM5 — down to an accessible price. The ANC performance is genuinely impressive: it knocks out low-frequency rumble from planes, trains, and HVAC systems in a way that noticeably changes your environment. Sony's Ambient Sound Mode lets you pass through external audio without removing the headphones.

Sound quality is clean, balanced, and detailed — Sony's house sound tuning tilts slightly warm without being bass-heavy. The WH-CH720N weighs just 192g, making it one of the lightest over-ear ANC headphones available. Battery life runs 35 hours with ANC on. Multipoint connection lets you pair to two devices simultaneously — switching from your laptop to your phone happens automatically. At $100, this is the ANC headphone we'd recommend to a friend without hesitation.

#2 — Soundcore Q45: Best Budget ANC

The Soundcore Q45 at around $60 is one of the most impressive value plays in audio. For $60, it delivers hybrid ANC (dual microphones, feedforward + feedback algorithm), 50 hours of battery life with ANC on — which is extraordinary — and a foldable design that makes it genuinely portable. The soundstage is wide for the price point, with a slight bass boost that suits pop, hip-hop, and electronic music well.

ANC performance is good — not class-leading, but meaningfully better than no noise cancellation. It handles constant low-frequency noise well (office HVAC, city ambient sound) and reduces high-frequency distractions enough to notice the difference. If your top priority is maximum battery life and you want ANC at the lowest possible price, the Q45 is unbeatable. The 50-hour battery means you can go a week or more between charges with normal use.

#3 — JBL Tune 770NC: Best for Commuters

The JBL Tune 770NC at around $80 is purpose-built for daily commuting. JBL's Adaptive ANC automatically adjusts noise cancellation based on your environment — a feature previously found only on premium headphones. On the subway or bus it cranks the cancellation up; in a quieter office it backs off to preserve battery and reduce the pressure sensation. The result is a more natural wearing experience throughout the day.

JBL's signature sound signature is warm and punchy — ideal if you listen to a lot of hip-hop, R&B, or electronic music and prefer elevated bass response. Battery life is a strong 44 hours with ANC on. The fold-flat design and included carrying pouch make it compact for daily carry. Call quality from the dual microphones is clear enough for work calls. For a commuter who changes environments frequently throughout the day, the adaptive ANC alone makes the JBL Tune 770NC worth the extra $20 over the Soundcore Q45.

What to Look For

ANC Technology: Feedforward, Feedback, and Hybrid

Feedforward ANC uses external microphones to sample noise before it reaches your ear — fast but can struggle with unpredictable sounds. Feedback ANC monitors what actually reaches your ear and corrects it — better at adapting but with a slight delay. Hybrid ANC combines both and is what all four headphones on this list use. It's the most effective approach available. Adaptive ANC (as in the JBL Tune 770NC) goes further by automatically adjusting cancellation strength to your environment.

Battery Life

ANC drains battery faster than standard playback mode. The figures above are all measured with ANC enabled — look for this distinction when comparing specs, as some manufacturers quote battery life without ANC. For daily commuting, 30+ hours with ANC is comfortable for a week of use between charges. The Soundcore Q45's 50 hours is exceptional and genuinely means charging once a week or less.

Multipoint Bluetooth

Multipoint allows one pair of headphones to maintain simultaneous Bluetooth connections to two devices — typically your laptop and phone. When your phone rings while you're listening on your laptop, the headphones automatically switch to handle the call without manually disconnecting and reconnecting. All four headphones on this list support multipoint, which is now the baseline expectation for any serious wireless headphone.

✓ Our Verdict

Best Overall: Sony WH-CH720N

For most people, the Sony WH-CH720N at $100 is the right answer — it has the best ANC of any headphone under $150, weighs almost nothing, and sounds great. On a tighter budget, the Soundcore Q45 at $60 is remarkable value and its 50-hour battery is genuinely hard to match. Commuting daily through multiple environments? The JBL Tune 770NC's Adaptive ANC will serve you better over a long day. If audio quality is paramount, the EarFun Wave Pro's LDAC support and tuning are the best in class at this price.
