Best Toaster Ovens Under $100 in 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 8, 2026 8 min read

A toaster oven under $100 can replace your microwave for reheating, toast bread better than a toaster, and bake small batches without heating a full-size oven. Modern models at this price even include air fry modes.

We focused on toaster ovens that offer the best combination of cooking performance, internal capacity, and heat-up speed at the $100-or-less price point.

What to Look For

  • Interior Capacity: Look for 0.8–1.2 cubic feet to fit a 12-inch pizza or a 9×13 baking dish. Smaller models limit what you can cook.
  • Cooking Functions: Toast, bake, and broil are the essential three. Air fry is a bonus at this price — verify actual performance rather than assuming equivalence to a standalone air fryer.
  • Preheat Speed: A toaster oven should reach baking temp in 5 minutes or less. Long preheat defeats the purpose of using it instead of a full oven.
  • Convection Fan: A convection setting circulates hot air for faster, more even baking and crispier results. Increasingly common even at budget price points.
  • Controls: Mechanical dials are simple and reliable. Digital controls offer more precision — confirm the minimum timer increment is fine enough for toast.
