Best Car Jump Starters in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 24, 2026 8 min read

A portable jump starter in your glovebox or trunk means a dead battery never strands you again. Modern lithium jump starters fit in a jacket pocket, deliver enough current to start a V8 truck, and double as a USB power bank for your phone. They've made roadside assistance membership optional for dead-battery scenarios.

We evaluated peak amperage, battery capacity, safety features (spark-proof clamps, reverse polarity protection), and ease of use for these four top picks.

What to Look For

  • Peak vs. cranking amps: Peak amps is the highest burst the unit delivers. Cranking amps (CA) is the sustained current — more important for cold-weather starting. Match to your engine size.
  • Safety features: Look for spark-proof clamp technology (NOCO's patented system), reverse polarity protection, and short-circuit protection. Cheap jump starters without these can damage electrical systems.
  • Battery capacity: Measured in mAh — also determines how many jump-starts per charge (usually 20–40) and how useful the built-in USB charging ports are.
  • Engine compatibility: Check the manufacturer's rating — most units specify max engine displacement they can start. NOCO GB40 handles 6L gas or 3L diesel engines.
