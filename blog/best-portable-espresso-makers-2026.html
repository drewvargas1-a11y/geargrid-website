Best Portable Espresso Makers of 2026

By GearGrid Staff September 9, 2026 8 min read

Real espresso doesn't require a countertop machine — the best portable espresso makers produce genuine 9-bar extraction pressure in a device that fits in a bag. Whether you're traveling, camping, or just tired of bad office coffee, these tools deliver a proper shot anywhere.

What to Look For

  • Pressure: True espresso needs 9 bars of pressure. Manual pumps (Wacaco, Flair) achieve this by hand; battery-powered units use a motor. Anything below 7 bar makes strong coffee, not espresso.
  • Capacity: Single-shot devices (30ml) are lighter and simpler. Double-shot (60ml) units are heavier but more practical for cappuccinos or longer drinks.
  • Grounds vs. Pods: Ground coffee gives better extraction and lower cost per cup. Nespresso-compatible pod adapters add convenience but reduce flexibility.
  • Weight: For backpacking, aim under 300g. Travel and office use can tolerate 400–700g for better build quality and capacity.
  • Heat Source: Manual devices need a separate kettle or thermos of hot water. All-in-one battery models heat water internally — the trade-off is battery life and weight.
