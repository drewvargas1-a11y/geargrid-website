Best Mosquito Repellents in 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 4, 2026 8 min read

Mosquito repellents reduce the risk of West Nile virus, Zika, dengue, and the misery of itchy bites. In 2026 the most effective options range from DEET-based sprays (the gold standard for duration) to picaridin (gentler, no plastic damage) to IR3535 (gentle enough for infants) to Thermacell's area-based butane-powered dispensers that protect a 15-foot zone without applying anything to skin.

We evaluated protection duration, active ingredient safety, ease of application, and use case (skin vs. area protection) across these four picks.

What to Look For

  • DEET: Most extensively studied repellent — effective at 15–30% concentration for 4–8 hours. 30% DEET is the CDC-recommended maximum for most activities. Damages plastics, synthetics, and some watch bands. Safe for adults and children over 2 months when used as directed.
  • Picaridin: Comparable effectiveness to DEET at 20% concentration, doesn't damage plastics or synthetics, odorless, and less greasy. Better tolerated by skin-sensitive individuals. The alternative to DEET recommended by CDC.
  • IR3535: Gentler than DEET — safe for infants, pregnant women, and sensitive skin. Shorter duration (2–4 hours) but effective for most outdoor activities.
  • Area protection: Thermacell dispenses allethrin (synthetic pyrethroid) from a heated mat — protects a 15-foot zone without skin application. Best for sitting on a deck or campsite; not practical for hiking.
  • Permethrin: Applied to clothing, gear, and tents — not skin. Kills and repels mosquitoes and ticks on contact. Lasts 6+ washings. Used in combination with skin repellent for maximum protection in high-bug environments.
