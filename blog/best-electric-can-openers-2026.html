Best Electric Can Openers in 2026: No Sharp Edges, No Effort

By GearGrid Staff July 19, 2026 8 min read

Electric can openers do one thing — open cans — and the best ones do it without leaving jagged, razor-sharp lids that cut your fingers when you reach in. Smooth-edge models cut along the side of the can rather than the top, producing a smooth rim and a lid you can drop in the trash safely.

Here are the best electric can openers of 2026, ranked by how cleanly they cut, edge safety, motor reliability, and ease of cleaning for the components that contact food.

What to Look For

  • Smooth-edge vs. standard: Standard openers cut through the top of the lid, leaving a sharp metal edge. Smooth-edge openers cut along the side seam, leaving a smooth rim on both can and lid. Much safer for households with children.
  • One-touch vs. manual hold: One-touch models start and stop automatically when the can is opened. Manual hold models run only while you press the lever. One-touch is more convenient for regular use.
  • Blade material: Stainless steel cutting wheels last longer and resist rust. Clean after each use to prevent food buildup in the gear mechanism.
  • Clearance height: Most electric openers handle standard cans up to 7". Check clearance for tall cans (28 oz tomatoes) — some models jam on taller cans.
  • Left-handed models: Most electric openers are right-hand optimized. If you're left-handed, look for models specifically designed for both orientations.
