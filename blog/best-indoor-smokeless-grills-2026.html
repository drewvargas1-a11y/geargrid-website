Best Indoor Smokeless Grills in 2026: Grill Year-Round

By GearGrid Staff July 23, 2026 8 min read

Indoor smokeless grills make year-round grilling possible in apartments and kitchens where outdoor grilling isn't an option. The best ones produce real grill marks and sear temperatures above 450°F, with integrated smoke extraction systems that capture grease vapor before it reaches the room air.

Here are the best indoor smokeless grills of 2026 — from contact grills that cook both sides simultaneously to open-top grill plates that replicate outdoor cooking — ranked by actual temperature, smoke control, and cleanup ease.

What to Look For

  • Contact vs. open-top: Contact grills (George Foreman style) press down on food and cook both sides simultaneously — fast but compresses food and loses juices. Open-top grills cook one side at a time like an outdoor grill — better results for steaks and burgers but take longer.
  • Temperature ceiling: Real searing requires 450°F+. Many indoor grills cap out at 350–400°F, which steams rather than sears. Look for grills with verified high-temp sear zones (Ninja Foodi, PowerXL).
  • Smoke management: Truly 'smokeless' grills use a temperature-controlled drip tray below the grill plate that keeps grease below its smoke point. Splatter screens reduce smoke but don't eliminate it. Water in the drip tray also reduces smoke significantly.
  • Cooking surface area: 120–150 sq in handles 2 burgers or 2 chicken breasts. 200+ sq in fits a full 4-serving meal. For a family, look for 240 sq in+.
  • Removable plates: Removable non-stick grill plates are essential for cleanup. Fixed grill surfaces are a cleaning nightmare. Dishwasher-safe plates are the most convenient.
