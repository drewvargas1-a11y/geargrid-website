Best Instant Pots in 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 2, 2026 8 min read

Instant Pots and multi-cookers combine 7–14 cooking functions in one pot — pressure cooking that cuts cooking time by 70%, slow cooking, sautéing, steaming, making yogurt, and more. In 2026 the best models add an air fryer lid, sous vide, baking, and dehydrating — making them more versatile than ever while the core pressure cooking function that made them famous remains best in class.

We evaluated pressure cooking performance (seal quality, pressure buildup speed), capacity, build quality, and function depth across these four picks.

What to Look For

  • Size: 3 qt for 1–2 people, 6 qt for 2–4 people (the most popular), 8 qt for 6+ people or meal prepping. The inner pot must be at least 1/2 full for pressure cooking to work — overfilling above 2/3 is also prohibited.
  • Pressure levels: High pressure (typically 10–12 psi) is used for most recipes. Low pressure is used for eggs, fish, and delicate foods. Verify your model offers both.
  • Sealing ring: The silicone sealing ring absorbs food odors — keep two rings (one for savory, one for sweet) to prevent flavor transfer. Replacement rings are $8–12.
  • Saute function: In the Instant Pot itself, so you can brown meat before pressure cooking without dirtying a separate pan. Essential for good stew and soup base flavor.
  • Air fryer lid: Some models include an air fryer lid that turns the inner pot into an air fryer. Convenient but not as powerful as a standalone air fryer — best for crisping tops of dishes.
