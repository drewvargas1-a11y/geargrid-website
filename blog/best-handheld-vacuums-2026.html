Best Handheld Vacuums in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 10, 2026 9 min read

A handheld vacuum is the tool you reach for between full vacuum sessions — crumbs on the couch, pet hair on the stairs, mess in the car, spilled cereal at breakfast. The best ones in 2026 have enough suction to be genuinely useful without being so heavy that you avoid using them.

We evaluated suction power, battery life, weight, filter quality, and pet hair performance to find the best handheld vacuums available.

What to Look For

  • Suction power: Measured in AW (air watts) or kPa. Higher is better, but above 100AW you are in full-size territory.
  • Battery type: Removable batteries let you swap in a fresh one. Built-in batteries are more common at the budget end.
  • Filter: HEPA filtration traps fine particles. Important if anyone in your household has allergies or asthma.
  • Weight: Under 3 lbs is ideal for one-handed use. Over 4 lbs becomes tiring quickly.
  • Pet hair: Motorized brush tools make a significant difference for pet hair on upholstery and carpets.
