Best Laundry Hampers in 2026: Organize Every Room's Laundry

By GearGrid Staff July 23, 2026 8 min read

A laundry hamper's main job is to contain dirty laundry without becoming an eyesore or a source of odor — two things most cheap ones fail at within a month. The best hampers sort laundry as you drop it in, fit the space you have, and are easy to carry to the washing machine without the bag falling out.

Here are the best laundry hampers of 2026 across every use case: divided hampers for pre-sorting darks and lights, collapsible hampers for small apartments, and lidded hampers that keep laundry smells contained.

What to Look For

  • Divided vs. single: Divided hampers let you pre-sort laundry (darks/lights or colors/whites) as you generate it, which removes the sorting step on laundry day. Single hampers are simpler and take less floor space.
  • Collapsible vs. rigid: Collapsible fabric hampers fold flat when empty — good for small spaces. Rigid hampers (plastic, wood-frame) hold their shape and look more furniture-like. Rigid wins in closets; collapsible wins in tight rooms.
  • Ventilation: Mesh panels or open-weave fabric allow airflow to prevent musty odors. Solid fabric hampers with lids trap moisture and smell worse over time. Ventilated sides are the most practical choice for bedroom hampers.
  • Removable liner: A removable cloth liner lets you carry laundry to the machine without the entire hamper. Look for handles on the liner specifically, not just the hamper frame.
  • Capacity: One person generates 1–2 loads per week. A 2-person household needs 2.5+ cubic feet (roughly 50L) to last a week between laundry days. A 3-section divided hamper often covers a couple's sorting needs in one unit.
