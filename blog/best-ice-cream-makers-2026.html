Best Ice Cream Makers of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 10, 2026 8 min read

Homemade ice cream from a quality machine beats any store-bought pint — you control the ingredients, flavors, and richness. Modern ice cream makers range from simple bowl-freeze models that require pre-freezing to self-cooling compressor machines that churn back-to-back batches.

These are the best ice cream makers of 2026, from budget bowl-freeze models for occasional weekend use to commercial-grade compressor machines for ice cream enthusiasts.

What to Look For

  • Compressor vs. Bowl Freeze: Compressor machines have a built-in freezer — no pre-freezing required, and you can make multiple batches. Bowl-freeze models require 24 hours of freezing the bowl first.
  • Capacity: 1–1.5 quarts is standard for home use, serving 4–6 people. 2-quart models suit families or entertaining.
  • Churning Time: Most machines churn a batch in 20–40 minutes. Compressor machines run slightly slower but allow immediate second batches.
  • Attachments: Some stand mixer attachments (KitchenAid) convert to ice cream bowls — worth considering if you already own a compatible stand mixer.
  • Hardness Setting: Some machines offer soft-serve or hard-pack settings that affect final texture. Soft-serve mode stops earlier; hard-pack churns longer for firmer scoopable ice cream.
