Best 4K Streaming Devices of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 12, 2026 8 min read

4K streaming in 2026 is the baseline expectation for any new TV setup — and a dedicated 4K streaming device delivers faster navigation, better HDR performance, and more reliable streaming than built-in smart TV apps on most sets.

These are the best dedicated 4K streaming devices in 2026, with HDR10+, Dolby Vision, and Atmos support across a range of prices and platforms.

What to Look For

  • HDR Format Support: Look for HDR10+ (Samsung TVs) or Dolby Vision (LG, Sony) support. The best devices support both. Standard HDR10 is the baseline and universally supported.
  • 4K Content Availability: Netflix, Disney+, Apple TV+, and Amazon Prime Video all stream in 4K. Verify that the device supports your streaming services' 4K implementations — some require specific certifications.
  • Audio Pass-Through: Dolby Atmos and DTS:X pass-through requires compatible HDMI cable, AV receiver, and soundbar. Confirm your device supports audio formats your system can decode.
  • Voice Remote Features: Look for a remote that controls TV power and volume via HDMI-CEC — eliminating the need for your TV remote. Dedicated streaming buttons (Netflix, Prime) save navigation time.
  • App Store Depth: All major platforms support mainstream services. Check for niche apps your setup needs — some sports streaming and cable replacement apps are platform-specific.
