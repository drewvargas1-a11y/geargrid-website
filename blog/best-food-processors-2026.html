Best Food Processors in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 10, 2026 9 min read

A food processor handles the prep work that takes the most time in the kitchen — slicing, shredding, chopping, dough mixing, and pureeing. Once you stop doing these by hand, going back feels impossible. The best models in 2026 are faster, quieter, and easier to clean than they were five years ago.

We evaluated motor power, bowl capacity, blade quality, ease of cleaning, and noise to find the best food processors available.

What to Look For

  • Motor power: 600W handles most household tasks. 1000W+ is worth it for regular bread dough or large batch processing.
  • Bowl capacity: 7-cup is the minimum practical size for a family. 11-14 cup handles larger batches without reloading.
  • Discs vs blades: S-blade for chopping and pureeing. Slicing and shredding discs handle the repetitive tasks that justify owning a food processor.
  • Dishwasher safe parts: The bowl, blades, and discs should all be dishwasher safe. Hand-washing a food processor is the main reason people stop using them.
  • Feed tube size: Wide feed tubes let you process whole vegetables without pre-cutting. Worth prioritizing.
