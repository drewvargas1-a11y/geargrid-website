Best Weighted Blankets in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 24, 2026 8 min read

Weighted blankets work through deep pressure stimulation — the gentle, even pressure of a heavy blanket mimics the sensation of being held, which reduces cortisol and increases serotonin and melatonin. Studies support their effectiveness for anxiety, insomnia, ADHD, and sensory processing disorders. The ideal weight is approximately 10% of your body weight.

We evaluated weight distribution (glass beads vs. cotton fill), breathability, washability, construction quality, and value. These four picks cover every preference from lightweight cotton to luxury hand-knit designs.

What to Look For

  • Weight selection: Standard guidance is 10% of body weight — a 150 lb adult should try a 15 lb blanket. Too heavy causes discomfort; too light provides minimal benefit.
  • Fill type: Glass micro-beads are the standard — heavy, small, and quieter than plastic pellets. Bearaby uses chunky-knit cotton with no beads — heavier per layer but breathable.
  • Breathability: 100% cotton construction breathes better than polyester. Hot sleepers should avoid fleece-lined weighted blankets.
  • Washability: Most glass-bead blankets can be machine-washed in large-capacity washers (4.5 cu ft+). Check the weight limit on your washer — 20 lb blankets may exceed capacity.
  • Cover quality: Two-sided blankets offer different textures — one cool, one warm. Duvet-style removable covers are easier to wash than integrated designs.
