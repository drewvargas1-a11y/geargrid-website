Best Kitchen Scales in 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 6, 2026 8 min read

A kitchen scale produces consistently better baking results than volume measurements — flour packed tightly in a cup can weigh 30% more than flour scooped lightly, leading to dense, dry baked goods. In 2026 the best kitchen scales read in 1-gram increments (or 0.1g for coffee brewing), have a tare function for measuring multiple ingredients in the same bowl, and support multiple units (grams, ounces, pounds, milliliters) with a button press.

We evaluated measurement accuracy, maximum capacity, minimum weight readability, platform size, and display clarity across these four picks.

What to Look For

  • Precision: 1g increments are adequate for baking. 0.1g precision is needed for coffee brewing where 0.5g of coffee grounds makes a perceptible difference in a 250ml cup. Nutritional tracking falls between these needs.
  • Capacity: Most home kitchen scales max at 11 lbs (5 kg). Scales for weighing large batches of dough or full pots need 22 lb (10 kg) capacity.
  • Tare function: Tare resets the scale to zero with a bowl or container on it — measure directly in your mixing bowl without math. All modern scales include tare; check the button placement for convenience.
  • Platform material: Stainless steel is easy to clean and resists scratches. Tempered glass is elegant but can crack. Plastic is lightweight but shows scratches. Stainless is the most practical kitchen material.
