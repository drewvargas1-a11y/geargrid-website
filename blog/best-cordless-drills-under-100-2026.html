Best Cordless Drills Under $100 in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 18, 2026 9 min read

A good cordless drill under $100 handles everything from hanging pictures to assembling furniture to drilling through studs. The best options in 2026 come from major tool brands with excellent battery ecosystems, meaning the battery you buy today is compatible with future tools in the same brand's lineup.

We evaluated torque output, chuck quality, clutch settings, battery runtime, and kit value to find the best cordless drills under $100.

What to Look For

  • Battery voltage: 18V-20V drills provide the most versatile power for general household work. 12V drills are more compact and sufficient for light work. 20V handles drilling through hardwood and thicker studs that 12V struggles with.
  • Battery ecosystem: DeWalt, Milwaukee, Ryobi, and Makita all have extensive battery ecosystems. Buying within an ecosystem means your batteries work across jigsaws, sanders, circular saws, and other tools from the same brand.
  • Clutch settings: Multiple clutch settings prevent overdriving screws and stripping wood. More clutch settings give finer torque control. 16-24 settings are adequate for most home use.
  • Chuck size: A 1/2-inch chuck accepts larger drill bits than a 3/8-inch chuck. For general home use, 1/2-inch is the better choice. Keyless chucks are standard and preferred for quick bit changes.
  • Two-speed gearbox: High gear (speed 2) for drilling; low gear (speed 1) for driving screws with more torque. Two-speed drills are more versatile than single-speed designs.
