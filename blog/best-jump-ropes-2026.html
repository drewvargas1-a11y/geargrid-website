Best Jump Ropes of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 10, 2026 8 min read

Jump rope is one of the most calorie-efficient cardio tools available — a 10-minute session burns as many calories as an 8-minute mile run while improving coordination, footwork, and cardiovascular fitness.

From featherlight speed ropes for double unders to heavy weighted ropes for upper-body conditioning, these are the best jump ropes of 2026.

What to Look For

  • Rope Weight and Thickness: Speed ropes have thin, light cables for fast rotations. Weighted ropes (1–2 lbs) increase upper body and core engagement. Choose by training goal.
  • Handle Material and Grip: Ball-bearing handles rotate smoothly and reduce wrist fatigue. Foam grips improve comfort for long sessions. Avoid rigid plastic with no ergonomic shaping.
  • Cable Adjustability: Adjustable cables accommodate different heights. Size roughly: stand on the middle of the rope, handles should reach your armpits.
  • Ball Bearing Quality: Quality sealed ball bearings spin freely under any intensity and last for millions of jumps. Cheap bearings create drag and break down quickly.
  • Rope Material: PVC cables are durable and weather-resistant. Steel cables in a coating are the fastest option for speed and double-under training.
