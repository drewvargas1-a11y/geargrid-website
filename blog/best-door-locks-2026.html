Best Smart Door Locks in 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 6, 2026 8 min read

Smart door locks eliminate the need for physical keys — unlocking via keypad code, smartphone Bluetooth, fingerprint, or voice command. In 2026 the best models work without an internet connection for local unlocking (critical for reliability), send smartphone notifications when the door is unlocked, and integrate with Alexa, Google, and Apple HomeKit for voice access control.

We evaluated lock security rating, installation ease (deadbolt replacement vs. full rekeying), connectivity (Bluetooth, Z-Wave, Wi-Fi, Matter), and smart home integration across these four picks.

What to Look For

  • ANSI/BHMA security grade: Grade 1 is the highest residential security rating — required for front door locks. Grade 2 is for interior doors. Grade 3 is the lowest — avoid for exterior use. Schlage and Kwikset both offer Grade 1.
  • Installation: Most smart locks replace only the interior portion of an existing deadbolt — preserving the exterior appearance. Full replacement locks change everything including the key cylinder. Retrofit locks (August) slide onto the existing deadbolt from inside — the easiest installation (10 minutes).
  • Connectivity: Bluetooth: works locally without internet, low battery drain. Z-Wave/Zigbee: requires a hub but provides reliable smart home integration. Wi-Fi: works from anywhere with internet but drains battery 2–3x faster. Matter: future-proof universal standard.
  • Backup access: Always ensure the smart lock includes physical key backup — smart locks fail (dead battery, connectivity issue). Key access is the fail-safe.
  • Auto-lock: Automatically locks after a set interval (1–30 minutes). Essential for security — eliminates the "did I lock the door" concern.
