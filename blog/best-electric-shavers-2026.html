Best Electric Shavers in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 18, 2026 9 min read

The best electric shavers in 2026 deliver a genuinely close shave without the skin irritation of manual razors — especially for daily shavers or anyone with sensitive skin. The technology gap between entry-level and premium shavers is significant and immediately noticeable in real-world use.

We evaluated shave closeness, skin comfort, battery runtime, wet/dry versatility, and cleaning system quality to find the best electric shavers.

What to Look For

  • Foil vs. rotary: Foil shavers use oscillating blades under a flat perforated screen — better for fine, flat-lying hair and sensitive skin. Rotary shavers use three circular heads that follow facial contours — better for thick, coarse, or multi-directional hair growth.
  • Wet and dry shaving: Waterproof shavers work with shaving gel or foam in the shower for the most comfortable experience. Dry-only shavers are simpler and less expensive but leave more post-shave irritation on sensitive skin.
  • Motor speed and power: Higher cycles per minute (cpm) means more blade passes per second. Braun's Series 9 runs at 40,000 cpm — noticeably more thorough per stroke than lower-cpm models on dense stubble.
  • Cleaning station: An automatic cleaning and charging station rinses, lubricates, and dries the shaving head after every use — extending blade life and maintaining hygiene. Manual cleaning works but takes longer and is less thorough.
  • Battery life: 60-90 minutes of cordless runtime is the standard for quality electric shavers. Quick-charge features (5 minutes for one shave) are useful for travel situations.
