Best Laptop Stands in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 22, 2026 9 min read

A laptop stand raises your screen to eye level, reducing neck flexion and improving posture immediately. Paired with a separate keyboard and mouse, a laptop stand transforms a laptop into an ergonomic workstation setup for under $50. The best 2026 models adjust continuously for the precise angle your posture requires.

We evaluated height range, stability under load, cooling airflow, material quality, and portability to find the best laptop stands for home and travel use.

What to Look For

  • Height and angle range: Fixed-angle stands are cheaper but set your screen height once. Adjustable stands with a wide height range let you optimize for sitting and standing desk use without changing stands. 6-25 inches of height range covers most setups.
  • Stability under load: A stand that vibrates or sways when you type on the keyboard (if using the laptop keyboard) defeats the ergonomic purpose. Look for non-slip feet and a center-of-gravity design that handles a 17-inch laptop without wobble.
  • Airflow and cooling: Open-frame designs let air circulate under the laptop — important for thermally throttled laptops running demanding tasks. Solid-surface stands trap heat against the bottom of the laptop.
  • Material: Aluminum stands are sturdy, light, and look premium on a desk. Steel stands are heavier but often more stable. Plastic stands are the cheapest but tend to scratch laptop surfaces and flex.
  • Portability: Home-only use: stability matters more than weight. Remote workers: a folding or collapsible stand under 0.5 lbs is worth the slight stability compromise for carry-on convenience.
