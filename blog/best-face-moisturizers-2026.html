Best Face Moisturizers of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 13, 2026 8 min read

A face moisturizer is the single most important skincare product for maintaining skin health — it prevents transepidermal water loss, supports barrier function, and keeps skin looking plump and smooth. Choosing the right one for your skin type makes all the difference.

These are the best face moisturizers of 2026 across skin types, covering lightweight gels for oily skin, rich creams for dry complexions, and SPF-integrated options for daily sun protection.

What to Look For

  • Skin Type Match: Oily and combination skin benefits from gel or water-based formulas. Dry and sensitive skin thrives with richer cream formulations. Combination skin does best with a lightweight cream or oil-free lotion.
  • SPF Integration: Daily SPF 30+ sunscreen is the most evidence-backed anti-aging intervention. SPF moisturizers provide sun protection in one step — though dedicated sunscreen still outperforms for high-exposure days.
  • Key Ingredients: Hyaluronic acid attracts water into skin. Ceramides restore barrier function. Niacinamide reduces redness and pore appearance. Glycerin is an inexpensive, effective humectant in all formulas.
  • Fragrance: Fragrance-free moisturizers are strongly preferred for sensitive or reactive skin. Even natural fragrances (essential oils) are a common trigger for skin sensitivity.
  • Texture and Finish: Gel formulas absorb quickly and leave a matte finish. Cream formulas are richer and better for dry skin. Lotions sit between the two.
