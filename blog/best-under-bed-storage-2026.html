Best Under-Bed Storage in 2026: Use Every Inch of Space

By GearGrid Staff July 21, 2026 8 min read

The space under a bed is some of the most underused storage in any home — typically 6–12 inches of clearance that can hold seasonal clothes, extra bedding, shoes, or anything else that's cluttering a closet. The right storage solution makes this space accessible and keeps contents dust-free.

Here are the best under-bed storage solutions of 2026 — rolling bins for easy access, vacuum compression bags for seasonal clothes, and fabric organizers for everyday use.

What to Look For

  • Clearance first: Measure your bed's ground clearance before buying. Most standard bed frames clear 7–9 inches. Platform beds may clear only 4–5 inches. The storage container height must be less than your clearance.
  • Wheels vs. no wheels: Rolling storage glides in and out without lifting the mattress or straining your back. Non-wheeled bins require pulling, which is fine for seldom-accessed seasonal items.
  • Lids and dust protection: Open-top bins collect dust quickly. Zippered lids or snap-on lids keep contents clean. Look for breathable fabric lids for clothes (prevents musty odors) and solid lids for dust-sensitive items.
  • Vacuum compression bags: Vacuum bags compress bulky items (comforters, sweaters) to 1/4 their volume. Best for seasonal storage. The compression fades over 3–6 months as air slowly returns — re-vacuum annually.
  • Visibility: Clear-sided bins let you see contents without pulling everything out. Opaque bins need labels. For frequently accessed items, clear wins.
