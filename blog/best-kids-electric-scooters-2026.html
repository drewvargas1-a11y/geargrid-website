Best Electric Scooters for Kids in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 26, 2026 8 min read

A kids' electric scooter needs to be genuinely slower and safer than adult models — with reliable hand brakes, sturdy decks, and clear speed limits that parents can trust. In 2026 the best models include adjustable handlebars to grow with the child, app-based speed limiting, and kickstand designs that prevent tipping on stairs. They make neighborhood rides independent and fun without unsafe speeds.

We evaluated speed limits and controls, braking system, weight capacity, recommended age range, and build quality for these four top picks.

What to Look For

  • Age-appropriate speeds: Ages 6–8: 8–10 mph max. Ages 8–12: 10–15 mph max. Ages 12+: up to 15–18 mph with parental permission. Many models include parent-controlled speed limits via app.
  • Braking: Foot brakes (rear fender brake) are standard but slower. Hand brakes are faster and more intuitive for children with cycling experience. Best models include both.
  • Weight limit: Check the weight limit — entry-level kids' scooters often cap at 100–120 lbs. Older kids or heavier children need higher-rated models.
  • Battery indicator: A clear battery indicator prevents kids from riding until the battery dies far from home. LED indicators are better than single-color lights.
  • Deck width: Wider decks give young riders more stability. Narrow adult-style decks require more balance experience than most children under 10 have.
