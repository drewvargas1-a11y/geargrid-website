Best Outdoor Pizza Ovens of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 10, 2026 8 min read

A proper outdoor pizza oven reaches 900°F+ — far beyond any kitchen oven — and cooks a Neapolitan-style pizza in 60–90 seconds. The result is a blistered, leopard-spotted crust that no conventional oven can replicate.

From Ooni's lightweight propane runners to Gozney's premium stone-floor dome ovens, outdoor pizza ovens in 2026 range from $250 to $1,500+ with real performance differences across the range.

What to Look For

  • Fuel Type: Propane is the easiest — turn a dial and you're cooking in 15 minutes. Wood-fired produces more flavor complexity. Multi-fuel models offer both options.
  • Maximum Temperature: Target 850–950°F for true Neapolitan style. Lower temps produce longer cook times and less authentic results.
  • Baking Stone Material: Cordierite stone stores and releases heat consistently. Thicker stones take longer to heat but hold temp better between pizza launches.
  • Preheat Time: Propane ovens preheat in 15–20 minutes. Wood-fired ovens take 30–45 minutes. Plan accordingly when timing dinner.
  • Size and Portability: 12-inch cooking floor fits one pizza at a time; 16-inch allows larger pies. Portable ovens (under 25 lbs) travel to camping trips and events.
