Best Cold Brew Coffee Makers in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 14, 2026 8 min read

Cold brew is not iced coffee — it is coffee steeped in cold water for 12 to 24 hours, which extracts the caffeine and flavor while leaving most of the acidity and bitterness behind. The result is smooth enough to drink black, and a batch in the fridge replaces a week of $6 coffee-shop runs.

Every maker here works on the same principle; the differences are in filtration, capacity, mess, and how easy the thing is to live with. We evaluated brew smoothness, ease of use, cleanup, and durability to find the best cold brew coffee makers.

What to Look For

  • Concentrate vs ready-to-drink: Systems like Toddy and OXO brew a concentrate you cut with water or milk — one batch lasts a week or more. Pitcher-style makers brew ready-to-drink strength that fills the fridge faster.
  • Filtration: Fine steel mesh is easy and reusable; felt or paper filters catch more sediment for a cleaner cup at the cost of a little more handling.
  • Capacity: A 1-quart concentrate brewer covers one person for a week. Households of cold brew drinkers should go straight to 2-quart pitchers.
  • Fridge footprint: Cold brew lives in the refrigerator. Slim pitchers and stackable carafes earn their place; wide buckets fight you for shelf space.
  • Cleanup: Grounds disposal is the worst part of cold brew. Look for filters that empty cleanly and parts that are dishwasher safe.
