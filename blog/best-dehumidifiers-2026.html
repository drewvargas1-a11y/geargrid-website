Best Dehumidifiers in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 12, 2026 8 min read

Humidity above 60 percent is where problems start: musty smells, condensation on windows, mold in closets, and that sticky summer feeling indoors. A dehumidifier holds your space at the 45-50 percent sweet spot — and the difference in basements, bedrooms, and crawl spaces is immediate.

We evaluated moisture removal rates, drainage options, noise, and energy efficiency to find the best dehumidifiers for every space, from a 4,500 sq ft basement to a small bathroom.

What to Look For

  • Pint rating: Modern 50-pint units (rated under the 2019 DOE standard) match what older listings called 70-pint. A 50-pint unit handles most basements and whole floors; 20-22 pint units suit single rooms.
  • Drainage: Every unit has a bucket, but continuous drain matters: a gravity hose port empties into a floor drain, while a built-in pump can push water up into a sink — essential for basements without floor drains.
  • Coverage area: Manufacturer square footage assumes moderate dampness. For very damp spaces, size up — a bigger unit runs less often and lasts longer.
  • Humidistat control: Set a target humidity and the unit cycles automatically. Auto-restart after power outages matters for basements you do not check often.
  • Noise: Expect 47-53 dB on high. For bedrooms, look for compact compressor or peltier units that run quieter at the cost of capacity.
