Best Streaming Devices of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 8, 2026 8 min read

A streaming device transforms any TV with an HDMI port into a smart TV. Even if your TV already has built-in apps, a dedicated streaming device typically offers a faster, smoother interface with more frequent updates.

The market in 2026 is dominated by four platforms — Fire TV, Roku, Google TV, and Apple TV. Each has different strengths depending on your ecosystem and how you watch content.

What to Look For

  • 4K vs. 1080p: 4K HDR streaming is the current standard if you have a 4K TV. 1080p sticks cost less and still look great on older sets.
  • Platform Ecosystem: Apple TV integrates seamlessly with iPhone/iPad/Mac. Google TV is ideal for Android users. Roku is the most neutral. Fire TV ties into Amazon Prime and Alexa.
  • App Availability: Most major apps (Netflix, Disney+, Max, Hulu) are on every platform. Niche apps vary — check before buying if you use less common services.
  • Voice Remote: Every current streaming stick includes a voice remote. Check which assistant is built in (Alexa, Google, Siri) and whether it has hands-free voice activation.
  • Processing Speed: Faster processors mean faster app loads, smoother scrolling, and snappier channel changes. Budget sticks can feel sluggish with multiple apps running.
