Best Wrist Wraps of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 13, 2026 8 min read

Wrist wraps stabilize and support the wrist joint during heavy pressing movements — preventing hyperextension on heavy bench press, overhead press, and clean and jerk. They're one of the most underutilized strength training accessories that actually make a measurable difference.

These are the best wrist wraps of 2026 for every lifting style — from stiff powerlifting wraps for maximum support to flexible cotton wraps that don't restrict range of motion for Olympic lifting.

What to Look For

  • Stiffness: Stiffer wraps (powerlifting style) provide maximum support but reduce wrist mobility — best for max effort bench press. Flexible wraps allow more natural movement — better for Olympic lifts and CrossFit.
  • Length: 12-inch wraps suit most lifters for bench press and overhead movements. 18-inch and 24-inch wraps provide more support for powerlifters maxing out.
  • Material: Cotton/polyester blends are breathable and washable. Stiff wraps often use elastic or leather reinforcement for rigidity.
  • Closure: Velcro closure allows quick on/off between sets. Thumb loop positioning determines how the wrap loads — ensure the thumb loop is comfortable with your grip style.
  • Competition Legal: IPF and USAPL powerlifting competitions specify maximum 1-meter wrist wrap length. Check federation rules before competition.
