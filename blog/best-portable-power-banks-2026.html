Best Portable Power Banks in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 22, 2026 9 min read

A power bank with 20,000 mAh and 45W USB-C PD fits in a jacket pocket and can charge an iPhone 3-4 times or top off a MacBook Air — all without finding an outlet. The best 2026 power banks combine high capacity with the fast charging protocols that modern phones and laptops demand.

We evaluated actual mAh capacity (vs. rated), USB-C Power Delivery wattage, simultaneous charging performance, and size-to-capacity ratio to find the best portable power banks.

What to Look For

  • Capacity (mAh): 5,000 mAh provides 1-1.5 full phone charges. 10,000 mAh handles 2-3 charges. 20,000 mAh is the maximum easily flyable on commercial aircraft (TSA allows up to 26,800 mAh or 100Wh without declaration). 30,000 mAh+ requires checking airline limits.
  • USB-C Power Delivery (PD): 45W PD charges a laptop slowly but adequately for maintaining charge during use. 65W PD charges a laptop at near-wall-charger speeds. 100W PD handles most laptops including 16-inch MacBook Pro. Check your laptop's USB-C charging requirements before assuming any power bank will work.
  • Simultaneous charging: Better power banks maintain full speed on individual ports while multiple devices charge simultaneously. Budget power banks throttle to 12-18W split across all ports — useful but slow for multiple fast-charge devices.
  • Pass-through charging: Pass-through lets you charge the power bank and your device simultaneously from one wall outlet. Useful for overnight hotel charging — plug in the bank, connect your phone to the bank, both charge.
  • Display accuracy: LED indicator lights show rough capacity (25%/50%/75%/100%). A percentage display is more precise and better for knowing exactly how much reserve remains before leaving for a long day.
