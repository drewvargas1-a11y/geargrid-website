Best Carbon Monoxide Detectors in 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 6, 2026 8 min read

Carbon monoxide is colorless, odorless, and the leading cause of accidental poisoning deaths in the US. Every home with gas appliances, an attached garage, or a fireplace needs working CO detectors on every floor and near sleeping areas. In 2026 the best smart CO detectors send smartphone alerts when CO is detected — notifying you if levels rise while you're sleeping or away from home.

We evaluated sensor accuracy, alarm response time, smart connectivity, battery life, and combination CO/smoke detector options across these four picks.

What to Look For

  • Placement: CO detectors should be installed on every level of the home, within 10 feet of each sleeping area, and near but not directly adjacent to gas appliances. Install at outlet height — CO is close to air weight and distributes throughout the room rather than rising or sinking.
  • Sensor technology: Electrochemical sensors are more accurate and stable than older biomimetic (gel) sensors — most modern detectors use electrochemical. Check the spec sheet.
  • Alarm response time: UL2034 standard requires CO detectors to alarm at 400 PPM within 4–15 minutes. Lower concentrations (70 PPM) trigger alarm within 1–4 hours. A faster-responding detector reduces exposure time.
  • Combination CO + smoke: Combination detectors save installation space and reduce the number of devices. Ensure the combined unit meets UL2034 (CO) and UL217 (smoke) standards separately — some combination units are better at one than the other.
  • Smart alerts: Wi-Fi connected CO detectors send push notifications to your phone — alerting you when away. Essential for vacation homes, rental properties, and people who sleep deeply.
