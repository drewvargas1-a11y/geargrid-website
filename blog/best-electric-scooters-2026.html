Best Electric Scooters in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 8, 2026 9 min read

Electric scooters have matured from novelty tech into a genuine last-mile commuting solution. The best models in 2026 offer 20+ mile range, reliable braking, and folding mechanisms that make storage in an apartment or office genuinely practical.

We evaluated real-world range, build quality, braking performance, and portability to find the best electric scooters at every price point.

What to Look For

  • Range: Manufacturer claims are under ideal conditions. Expect 60-70% of the stated range in real-world use with hills and variable speed.
  • Max speed: 15-20mph is practical for most city commuters. Higher speed scooters may be regulated in your city.
  • Brakes: Disc brakes on both wheels are the safest option. Electronic-only braking is not sufficient at higher speeds.
  • Tire type: Pneumatic (air-filled) tires absorb bumps far better than solid tires. Essential for rough pavement.
  • IP rating: Look for IP54 or higher if you plan to ride in wet conditions.
