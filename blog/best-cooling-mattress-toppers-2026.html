Best Cooling Mattress Toppers in 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 4, 2026 8 min read

Hot sleepers overheat during the night — raising core body temperature and reducing time in deep and REM sleep stages. In 2026 the best cooling mattress toppers use gel-infused memory foam, copper-infused foam, phase-change material covers, or open-cell latex that absorbs and dissipates body heat rather than trapping it. A cooling topper costs $100–$300 and extends the life of a mattress by adding a fresh comfort layer.

We evaluated heat dissipation effectiveness, cooling material technology, comfort firmness, motion transfer, and value across these four picks.

What to Look For

  • Cooling mechanisms: Gel-infused foam absorbs heat initially but saturates with body heat within 30–60 minutes. Copper-infused foam conducts heat away continuously. Phase-change materials (PCM) absorb heat when you warm up and release it when you cool down. Open-cell latex and latex-alternative foams allow airflow rather than absorbing heat.
  • Thickness: 1–2 inch toppers add minor comfort adjustment. 2–3 inch toppers significantly change the feel of the mattress. 3+ inch toppers are essentially a new sleep surface. Most cooling toppers are 2–3 inches — enough to affect heat management without changing mattress support substantially.
  • Cover material: Cooling covers (Tencel, bamboo-derived Viscose, or PCM covers) add a first-contact cooling layer — the immediate cool-to-touch feeling. Covers wash separately — a practical hygiene advantage over toppers alone.
  • Compatibility: Toppers work on any mattress. Memory foam toppers add softness to firm mattresses. Firm latex toppers add support to soft mattresses. Choose based on what your current mattress lacks.
