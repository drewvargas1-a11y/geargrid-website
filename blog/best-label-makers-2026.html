Best Label Makers of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 12, 2026 8 min read

A label maker transforms a cluttered pantry, garage, or filing system into an organized, easy-to-navigate space in an afternoon. Modern label makers connect to your smartphone, print at multiple sizes, and produce laminated labels that last for years.

These are the best label makers of 2026, from simple handheld keyboard models to Bluetooth-connected thermal printers that pair with design apps on your phone.

What to Look For

  • Label Width: Standard widths: 6mm (small), 9mm (medium), 12mm (versatile), 18mm (large). Wider labels are more readable but use more tape. Look for models that accept multiple tape widths.
  • Thermal vs. Ink: Thermal label makers use heat-sensitive tape with no ink cartridges — the tape is the consumable. Much cheaper to run than ink-based printers.
  • Tape Compatibility: Proprietary tape is often more expensive. Some Brother P-Touch models accept third-party TZe tapes that cost 40–60% less than genuine tape.
  • App Connectivity: Bluetooth label makers pair with iOS/Android apps for full design control — fonts, icons, barcodes, and QR codes. Standalone keyboard models are faster for quick single labels.
  • Printing Speed: Measured in mm/second. Faster printing (20mm/s+) matters if you're labeling hundreds of items in one session.
