Best Luggage Tags in 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 6, 2026 8 min read

A visible, durable luggage tag helps airport staff return lost bags and helps you identify your bag on the carousel among dozens of identical black rolling suitcases. In 2026 smart luggage tags with AirTag slots combine the visibility of a traditional tag with the GPS-like location tracking of Apple's Find My network — a meaningful upgrade for checked luggage that gets misrouted by airlines.

We evaluated visibility (color and material), durability, privacy protection (hiding personal info), AirTag integration, and value across these four picks.

What to Look For

  • Privacy flap: Airport-visible tags display your contact information — including your name and phone number — to anyone passing the carousel. A privacy flap that covers personal info until opened is a simple security feature.
  • Material: Leather tags are durable and look premium but crack in cold. Silicone is weatherproof, extremely durable, and flexible. Aluminum is the most durable but adds weight.
  • Bright vs. standard colors: A bright-colored or uniquely marked tag makes your bag immediately identifiable on the carousel — reducing the chance of bag mix-ups and carousel theft. Distinctive tags also mark your bag for customs officials.
  • AirTag integration: AirTag slots hold an AirTag inside the tag housing — tracking your bag's location on Apple Find My. Particularly useful for international checked luggage where misrouting is more common.
