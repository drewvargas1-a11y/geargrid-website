Best Travel Pillows in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 16, 2026 9 min read

A good travel pillow makes the difference between arriving rested and arriving with a stiff neck. The best options in 2026 combine ergonomic support with neck stability — not just a U-shaped ring filled with cheap foam.

We evaluated neck support, pack size, washability, and real-world comfort on long-haul flights and road trips to find the best travel pillows available.

What to Look For

  • Support type: Memory foam provides consistent support but packs large. Inflatable pillows pack to almost nothing but require inflation. Microbead fills pack medium and feel similar to memory foam.
  • Chin support: Without chin support, your head drops forward as you fall asleep. Look for models with a front strap, chin support panel, or ergonomic shape that holds the chin up.
  • Height adjustment: Fixed height pillows fit some necks perfectly and others poorly. Adjustable inflation or removable inner layers solve this. One-size-fits-all is rarely one size fits all.
  • Washability: The cover should be removable and machine washable. After a long international flight, you will want to clean it.
  • Packing method: Compressible pillows pack into their own carry bag. Some attach to luggage via snap loops. If you carry on only, pack size matters enormously.
