Best Ultrawide Monitors in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 16, 2026 9 min read

An ultrawide monitor replaces two monitors without the bezel gap in the middle, and the 21:9 aspect ratio gives you more horizontal real estate for spreadsheets, timelines, code, and gaming than any standard 16:9 panel.

We tested color accuracy, refresh rates, panel uniformity, USB-C connectivity, and gaming performance to find the best ultrawide monitors available in 2026.

What to Look For

  • Panel type: IPS ultrawides have better color accuracy and viewing angles. VA panels offer higher contrast ratios — good for dark scenes. OLED ultrawides have arrived but remain significantly more expensive.
  • Resolution: 3440x1440 (WQHD) is the standard ultrawide resolution. It requires a capable GPU and is noticeably sharper than 2560x1080. For productivity, WQHD is worth the extra GPU demand.
  • Refresh rate: 34-inch WQHD ultrawides now commonly hit 144-165Hz, making them viable for gaming as well as work. 60Hz is acceptable for pure productivity use.
  • Connectivity: USB-C with Power Delivery (65W+) lets you connect a laptop with a single cable. Prioritize this if you switch between desktop and laptop regularly.
  • Curvature: 1800R curvature is aggressive and wraps your peripheral vision — great for gaming, potentially disorienting for work. 3440R is subtler and more neutral for document work.
