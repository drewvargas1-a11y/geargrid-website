Best Record Players of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 8, 2026 8 min read

Vinyl has never gone away — and in 2026, the selection of quality turntables is better than ever. Whether you're a first-time vinyl listener or an experienced audiophile expanding your rig, the right turntable makes a dramatic difference in how your records sound.

These are the best record players across every budget, from entry-level all-in-ones to serious belt-drive setups that reward further investment in cartridges and speakers.

What to Look For

  • Drive Type: Belt-drive turntables isolate the platter from motor vibration for better sound quality — the clear choice for most listeners. Direct-drive suits DJs needing torque and speed stability.
  • Built-in Preamp: A built-in phono preamp lets you connect directly to powered speakers or a receiver without a dedicated phono stage — essential for beginners.
  • Cartridge Quality: The cartridge and stylus determine sound quality more than anything else. Entry-level tables often accept cartridge upgrades down the line.
  • Speed Accuracy: Look for anti-skating adjustment and pitch control if you care about accurate playback. Budget all-in-ones often skip these.
  • USB Output: Handy for digitizing records to your computer. Not needed if you only want to listen.
