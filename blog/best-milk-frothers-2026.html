Best Milk Frothers of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 8, 2026 8 min read

A quality milk frother transforms your morning coffee from basic to barista-level. Whether you want the dense microfoam of a flat white or the airy froth of a cappuccino, the right frother makes a real difference.

From $10 handheld wands to $90 automatic frothers that heat and froth in one go, there's a model for every coffee habit and budget.

What to Look For

  • Frother Type: Handheld wands are cheap and effective. Automatic countertop frothers heat and froth automatically. Steam wands (on espresso machines) produce the finest microfoam.
  • Hot vs. Cold Frothing: Automatic frothers can typically heat milk and create foam at the same time, or froth cold milk for iced drinks. Confirm both modes are available if you make cold drinks too.
  • Milk Compatibility: Oat milk, almond milk, and soy milk all froth differently. Look for frothers rated for non-dairy milks if that's your primary milk.
  • Capacity: Most automatic frothers max out at 4–8 oz per batch. Measure your largest drink before buying to avoid multiple batches.
  • Cleanup: Milk residue dries fast and turns sticky. Non-stick interiors and dishwasher-safe carafes dramatically reduce cleanup hassle.
