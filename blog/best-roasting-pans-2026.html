Best Roasting Pans of 2026

By GearGrid Staff October 8, 2026 3 min read

A roasting pan is one of those tools you use a few times a year, and badly need when you do. A sturdy pan with a rack lifts the bird out of its drippings so heat circulates for crisper skin, and gives you a solid base for making gravy afterward.

We compared size, rack design, materials, and handles across stainless steel pans built for turkey season.

What to Look For

  • Size: A 16" pan handles a turkey of around 20 lb; measure your oven before buying anything larger.
  • V-rack: A V-shaped rack holds poultry upright and lifts it out of the drippings.
  • Material: Uncoated stainless steel is durable, oven- and stovetop-safe, and won't chip.
  • Wall height: Taller walls catch splatter and drippings; lower walls brown vegetables better.
  • Handles: Sturdy, wide handles matter when you're lifting a heavy, hot turkey.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size roasting pan do I need for a turkey?

A 16" pan fits most turkeys up to around 20 lb. Leave a couple of inches of clearance on each side of the oven for air circulation.

Do I need a rack in my roasting pan?

A rack lifts the bird out of its juices so the bottom browns instead of steaming, and makes lifting the turkey out easier.

Can you make gravy in a roasting pan?

Stainless pans can go on the stovetop, so you can deglaze the drippings right in the pan.

Is a disposable foil pan good enough?

It works in a pinch, but foil pans flex when lifted and brown less evenly. Support one on a baking sheet if you use it.
