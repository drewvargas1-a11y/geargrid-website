Best Electric Griddles in 2026: Breakfast for the Whole Table

By GearGrid Staff July 21, 2026 8 min read

An electric griddle gives you a large, evenly heated flat cooking surface that your stovetop pans can't replicate — great for pancake breakfasts, bacon strips, smash burgers, quesadillas, or cooking for a crowd without juggling multiple pans. The best ones heat evenly across the entire surface, which is the key spec most buyers overlook.

Here are the best electric griddles of 2026, ranked by cooking surface area, temperature consistency across zones, non-stick durability, and cleanup ease.

What to Look For

  • Surface area: A 200 sq in surface fits 6 pancakes or 8 strips of bacon at once. The larger 300+ sq in models feed 6–8 people simultaneously. Match size to how many people you typically cook for.
  • Temperature consistency: Cheap griddles run 50–75°F hotter in the center than the edges. Quality griddles maintain ±10°F across the whole surface. This matters for even pancakes and avoiding burnt spots.
  • Temperature range: 250–400°F covers all griddle cooking. Higher max temps (450°F) are useful for smash burgers and searing. Look for a precise thermostat, not just a dial with no feedback.
  • Non-stick coating: PTFE (Teflon) coatings are the most non-stick. Ceramic coatings are more durable but require more oil. Replace the griddle (or re-coat) when non-stick starts peeling.
  • Cleanup: Removable, dishwasher-safe plates are the biggest convenience upgrade over fixed surfaces. Drip trays catch grease — essential for bacon and burgers.
