Best Smart Displays in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 16, 2026 9 min read

Smart displays have evolved from glorified recipe holders into central hubs for controlling your entire home. The best ones in 2026 handle video calls, smart home dashboards, ambient photo frames, and streaming all in one.

We tested screen brightness, voice assistant accuracy, smart home compatibility, and daily usefulness to rank the best smart displays available.

What to Look For

  • Screen size and placement: 8-10 inch screens are ideal for kitchens and bedside tables. 10-15 inch displays work better as living room hubs or home office pieces.
  • Smart home hub built-in: The best displays include a Zigbee, Thread, or Matter hub, so you can pair smart bulbs and sensors directly without a separate bridge.
  • Camera quality: For video calls, look for at least 12MP with auto-framing. Some displays track you as you move around the kitchen.
  • Voice assistant: Alexa and Google Assistant both have strong ecosystems. Alexa leads in smart home device compatibility; Google Assistant is better at answering general questions.
  • Display brightness: A display in a well-lit kitchen needs at least 400 nits to stay readable. Cheap models wash out completely in daylight.
