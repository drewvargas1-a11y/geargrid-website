Best Travel Backpacks in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 30, 2026 8 min read

A travel backpack that fits as a carry-on eliminates checked luggage fees (typically $30–$60 per flight), removes the wait at baggage claim, and lets you move through airports and cities without dragging wheeled luggage over cobblestones. In 2026 the best travel backpacks are engineered specifically for airline carry-on compliance: 40–45L capacity in exactly the right dimensions to fit in overhead bins.

We evaluated carry-on size compliance, packing organization, laptop protection, harness comfort for walking, and weather resistance across these four picks.

What to Look For

  • Carry-on dimensions: Most airlines accept 45L bags up to approximately 22x14x9 inches. Budget carriers (Spirit, Frontier) restrict personal items to bags under 18x14x8 inches. Verify airline policy before traveling.
  • Suitcase vs. backpack style opening: Clamshell opening (like a suitcase) provides full access to all packed items. Backpack-style top-loading requires unpacking to reach bottom items. For travel packing: clamshell wins.
  • Laptop access: A dedicated TSA-friendly laptop compartment accessible without opening the main bag saves time at security. Look for a pass-through sleeve on the back of the bag.
  • Hip belt: A removable hip belt distributes 30–40% of backpack weight to the hips — dramatically reducing shoulder strain for bags over 25 lbs. Essential for heavy packing or long airport transfers.
  • Packable vs. structured: Structured backpacks maintain shape when packed — better for organization. Packable designs compress small when empty — useful for day trips from your hotel.
