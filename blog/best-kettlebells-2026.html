Best Kettlebells of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 10, 2026 8 min read

Kettlebells build explosive power, cardiovascular endurance, and full-body strength simultaneously — no other piece of gym equipment matches their efficiency per pound of iron. A single 35-lb kettlebell can provide a complete workout anywhere.

Here are the best kettlebells in 2026 — from individual cast iron classics to adjustable models that replace a full rack, and competition-grade bells for serious athletes.

What to Look For

  • Cast Iron vs. Competition Grade: Cast iron bells vary in size by weight. Competition bells maintain a fixed shell size regardless of weight — preferred by serious athletes for consistent form.
  • Handle Diameter: 16–18mm handles suit most people. Wider handles (20mm+) found on heavier bells can cause forearm skin abrasion on high-rep swings for new users.
  • Coating Type: Powder coat provides grip without chipping. Vinyl-coated bells protect floors but reduce grip feel. Bare cast iron develops a natural grip over time.
  • Weight Selection: Women typically start with 18–26 lbs (8–12 kg). Men typically start with 35–44 lbs (16–20 kg). When a weight becomes easy, move up 10–15%.
  • Adjustable vs. Fixed: Adjustable kettlebells save space but add mechanical complexity. Fixed bells are simpler and more durable — worth buying individual weights if you'll use them heavily.
