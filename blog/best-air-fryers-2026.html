Best Air Fryers in 2026: Every Budget Ranked

GearGrid Staff May 28, 2026 8 min read

Air fryers have become one of the most-used kitchen appliances in American homes — and for good reason. Rapid hot air circulation cooks food faster than a conventional oven, uses 75–80% less oil than deep frying, and produces genuinely crispy results on everything from frozen fries to chicken wings to vegetables. The market has matured considerably, meaning you can get a legitimately great air fryer for under $50, and the premium options justify their price with larger capacity and smarter controls.

We analyzed thousands of real-world reviews and cross-referenced cooking performance data from America's Test Kitchen and Consumer Reports to find the best air fryers at every price point. These five picks cover compact budget options through large-family dual-basket models.

Our Top Picks

#1 — Ninja AF101 4-Quart: Best Overall

The Ninja AF101 at around $90 is the air fryer to beat at this price. Ninja's wide-temperature range (105°F–400°F) lets you dehydrate food at the low end and crisp at the high end, giving it more versatility than competitors stuck at 175°–400°F. The 4-quart basket comfortably fits a whole rotisserie chicken or 2 lbs of fries — enough for 2–3 people. It preheats in under 3 minutes and the non-stick ceramic-coated basket releases cleanly and goes straight in the dishwasher.

Four preset cooking functions (Air Fry, Roast, Reheat, Dehydrate) cover every use case without an overcrowded control panel. Ninja's build quality is noticeably better than budget alternatives — the basket handle feels solid, the fan is quieter, and the results are consistently even across the entire basket. For a household of 1–3 people who want one appliance that does it all well, the AF101 is the standard recommendation.

#2 — Gourmia 6-Quart Digital Window: Best Budget Large

The Gourmia 6-Quart Digital Window Air Fryer at just $50 is the best budget air fryer for families that need more than a 3–4 qt basket. The see-through window on the basket door is a genuinely useful feature — you can monitor cooking progress without opening the basket and releasing heat, which improves consistency. Twelve cooking presets cover chicken, steak, fish, fries, vegetables, and more, all controlled from a clear digital touchscreen.

At $50 for 6 quarts, the value is exceptional. Gourmia's budget models don't match Ninja's build quality or temperature precision, but for everyday cooking tasks — frozen foods, wings, vegetables, reheating leftovers — it produces very good results. This is the pick for larger households or anyone who makes meals for 4+ people regularly.

#3 — PowerXL Vortex Pro: Best Mid-Range Value

The PowerXL Vortex Pro at around $60 hits a sweet spot between the budget Gourmia and the premium Ninja. It features a 5-quart basket, 8 one-touch cooking presets, and PowerXL's TurboSpeed fan that circulates heat aggressively for faster, crispier cooking results. The temperature range reaches 400°F and the preheat time is fast. The basket is dishwasher-safe and the non-stick coating releases food cleanly.

What separates the Vortex Pro is the fan power — PowerXL's high-speed circulation produces noticeably crispier results than same-priced Gourmia models on foods like chicken skin, pork belly, and thick-cut fries where texture is critical. For $60, it's the best cooking performance per dollar on this list outside of the Ninja.

#4 — Gourmia 3-Quart Compact: Best for Small Kitchens

The Gourmia 3-Quart Digital Air Fryer at just $40 is the right pick when counter space is limited. At roughly the footprint of a large toaster, it fits easily in small apartments, dorm rooms, and RVs. The 3-quart basket handles single-serving or couple-sized portions of fries, wings, or vegetables without issue, and the guided cooking prompts on the display walk you through time and temperature settings for common foods — useful for first-time air fryer users.

You give up capacity and some temperature precision at this price, but if you're cooking for one or two people and counter space is the constraint, the Gourmia 3qt is the most sensible starting point. It's also the easiest to recommend as a gift for college students, small household setups, or anyone testing the air fryer concept before committing to a bigger model.

The Cosori 6-Quart TurboBlaze at around $130 is the premium pick — the air fryer you buy when you want the best cooking performance and don't want to think about it.
