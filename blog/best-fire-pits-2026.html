Best Fire Pits of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 10, 2026 8 min read

A fire pit turns any backyard into an outdoor living room. Modern fire pits range from simple wood-burning steel bowls to sophisticated smokeless designs and push-button propane pits that require zero maintenance.

These are the best fire pits of 2026 — from affordable starter pits to premium smokeless models that actually keep smoke out of your eyes while you sit around the fire.

What to Look For

  • Fuel Type: Wood-burning is atmospheric and traditional; propane is convenient but less primal. Smokeless fire pit systems use secondary combustion to dramatically reduce smoke — a recent innovation worth the premium.
  • Size and Heat Output: Larger pits (30"–36") generate more heat and accommodate more people. Smaller tabletop models (18"–24") are great for small patios and apartments.
  • Material and Durability: Heavy-gauge steel resists warping under high heat. Powder-coated finishes extend outdoor life significantly versus bare steel.
  • Portability: Some fire pits include carrying bags for camping use. Propane pits are inherently more portable. Heavy cast iron pits stay put season to season.
  • Safety Features: Spark screens, cool-to-touch legs, and cover lids are important if children or pets will be nearby.
