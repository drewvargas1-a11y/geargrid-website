Best Gifts for Grandparents of 2026

By GearGrid Staff November 10, 2026 3 min read

Grandparents usually say they don't need anything, but gifts that keep them close to family or make everyday life more comfortable are always welcome.

We picked four easy-to-use gifts that don't require tech skills to enjoy.

What to Look For

  • Ease of use: Choose gifts that work with little or no setup on their end.
  • Family connection: Photo frames and smart displays keep grandkids in view.
  • Comfort: Heat and reading comfort matter more with age.
  • Readable screens: Larger text and adjustable lighting help aging eyes.
  • Setup help: Set devices up before you wrap them if you can.
