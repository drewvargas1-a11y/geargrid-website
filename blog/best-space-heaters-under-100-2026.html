Best Space Heaters Under $100 in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 28, 2026 8 min read

A space heater lets you heat only the rooms you're using — keeping the rest of the house at a lower temperature while staying comfortable where you are. In 2026 the best models under $100 combine ceramic PTC heating elements (which self-regulate temperature to prevent overheating) with tip-over protection and auto-shutoff at 90°F surface temperature — making them meaningfully safer than older coil designs.

We evaluated heating output, safety certifications, noise level, heat distribution method, and value to find the best under $100.

What to Look For

  • Ceramic PTC vs. infrared vs. oil-filled: Ceramic PTC heats the air and circulates it — best for rooms where you move around. Infrared heats people and objects directly — best for stationary use at a desk. Oil-filled radiators retain heat longer after being turned off.
  • Wattage: 1500W is the maximum for standard 120V household outlets — the practical limit for any portable heater. At 1500W a space heater runs efficiently on a standard 15-amp circuit.
  • Tip-over protection: Required safety feature — auto-shutoff if the unit tips over. All products here include it, but verify certification (ETL, UL) rather than trusting brand claims alone.
  • Thermostat accuracy: Built-in thermostats maintain room temperature at your set point and cycle on/off — reducing electricity use compared to running at full power constantly.
  • Noise level: Ceramic fan heaters run at 45–50dB at high. Infrared and oil-filled heaters are near-silent. For office or bedroom use, prioritize low-noise models.
