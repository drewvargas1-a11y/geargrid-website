Best Gifts for Golfers of 2026

By GearGrid Staff November 10, 2026 3 min read

Golfers love anything that helps them score better, from accurate yardages to premium balls and practice at home.

We picked four gifts for every level of golfer.

What to Look For

  • Skill level: Beginners benefit from GPS watches; serious golfers love rangefinders.
  • Tournament rules: Slope features must be switchable off for tournament play.
  • Ball preference: Many golfers have a favorite ball; Pro V1 is a safe bet.
  • Winter practice: Putting mats keep skills sharp in the off-season.
  • Budget: Balls are a safe small gift; rangefinders are a splurge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are rangefinders legal in tournaments?

Many events allow distance devices with slope disabled. Check local rules.

GPS watch or rangefinder?

GPS watches give quick yardages; rangefinders give exact distances to targets.

Are prior-generation Pro V1s still good?

Yes, they're a premium ball and often cost less.

Do putting mats help?

They build stroke consistency, especially in winter.
