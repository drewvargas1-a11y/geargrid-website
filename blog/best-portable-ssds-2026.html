Best Portable SSDs in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 28, 2026 8 min read

Portable SSDs are the right tool when speed and durability matter more than cost per gigabyte. They transfer a 4K video file in seconds instead of minutes, survive a drop that would kill a spinning hard drive, and store in a shirt pocket. In 2026 USB 3.2 Gen 2 SSDs deliver 1000–2000 MB/s read speeds — fast enough to edit 4K footage directly from the drive without a local copy.

We evaluated sequential read/write speeds, shock and drop resistance, enclosure quality, and price per GB across these four picks.

What to Look For

  • USB interface: USB 3.2 Gen 2 (10Gbps) is the current standard — delivers up to 1000 MB/s. USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 (20Gbps) reaches 2000 MB/s. Thunderbolt 4 SSDs can hit 3000+ MB/s but require Thunderbolt ports.
  • Durability ratings: Look for IP55+ water and dust resistance and a tested drop resistance of at least 6 feet. The Samsung T7 Shield has IP65 and 9.8-foot drop protection.
  • Capacity options: 500GB handles most current project files. 1TB is the practical sweet spot. 2TB+ for large media libraries.
  • NVMe vs. SATA internally: Most portable SSDs use NVMe internally now — this is what enables speeds above 500 MB/s. SATA-based enclosures are limited to ~550 MB/s regardless of USB interface.
