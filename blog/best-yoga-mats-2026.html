Best Yoga Mats in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 20, 2026 9 min read

A good yoga mat grips consistently on both the mat-to-floor and body-to-mat surfaces, provides enough cushioning for joint protection without being so soft that balance poses become unstable. The best 2026 yoga mats cover all three requirements while being durable enough for daily practice over years.

We evaluated grip performance (dry and sweaty), cushion thickness, weight, eco-material quality, and real-world durability to find the best yoga mats for every style of practice.

What to Look For

  • Cushion thickness: 4mm is the standard balance between cushion and stability — enough for joints without feeling like a mattress in balance poses. 6mm provides more comfort for restorative yoga. 3mm or less is best for travel where pack weight matters.
  • Grip materials: Natural rubber mats grip better than PVC when wet — critical for hot yoga. PVC mats grip better when dry and are more durable over time. TPE mats are eco-friendly and grip adequately but degrade faster than rubber or PVC.
  • Open-cell vs. closed-cell surface: Open-cell rubber absorbs sweat for superior wet grip. Closed-cell PVC repels moisture — better for low-sweat practices but slippery in hot yoga.
  • Weight and portability: Studio mats (Manduka PRO) weigh 7+ lbs — too heavy to carry unless you keep them at the studio. Travel mats at 2-3 lbs fold or roll compact for bags. Pick based on where you primarily practice.
  • Alignment lines: Grid or alignment markers help beginners place hands and feet consistently. Experienced practitioners rarely need them, but they improve learning speed for newer students.
