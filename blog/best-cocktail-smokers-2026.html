Best Cocktail Smoker Kits of 2026

By GearGrid Staff October 19, 2026 3 min read

A cocktail smoker adds a layer of wood smoke to an old fashioned, manhattan, or neat whiskey. Most kits use a small wooden chimney that sits on top of the glass, a few wood chips, and a refillable butane torch.

They make popular gifts for whiskey drinkers. We compared what each kit includes, how many wood flavors come in the box, and whether butane is included.

What to Look For

  • Butane: Most kits ship without butane because of shipping rules. Plan to buy a can separately.
  • Wood chip variety: Cherry, apple, oak, and hickory are the classics; more flavors give more to experiment with.
  • Torch quality: A refillable, adjustable torch with a lock is easier and safer to use.
  • Extras: Glasses, ice molds, and whiskey stones make a kit more complete as a gift.
  • Fit: Check that the smoker top fits your glasses; most fit standard rocks glasses.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you use a cocktail smoker?

Place a pinch of wood chips on the screen, set the smoker on the glass, light the chips with the torch, and let the smoke settle for 10 to 30 seconds before lifting it off.

Which wood is best for smoking whiskey?

Oak and hickory are bold; cherry and apple are sweeter and lighter. Many people start with cherry.

Is cocktail smoke safe?

Use food-grade wood chips made for smoking, work in a ventilated area, and keep the torch away from flammable items.

Can you smoke food with these kits?

Many work on cheese, nuts, and small dishes under a cloche, but they're designed mainly for drinks.
