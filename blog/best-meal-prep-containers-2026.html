Best Meal Prep Containers of 2026

By GearGrid Staff September 11, 2026 8 min read

Good meal prep containers need to survive the microwave, freezer, and dishwasher on repeat without staining, warping, or leaking in a bag. Glass wins on all three for most people, at the cost of some extra weight.

We compared compartment options, lid seal quality, and set size, since the right container count depends entirely on how many meals you're actually prepping per week.

What to Look For

  • Material: Borosilicate glass resists staining and odor absorption far better than plastic, and it's safe at higher oven temps.
  • Compartments: 1-compartment containers suit mixed bowls; 2-3 compartments keep proteins, carbs, and veggies separate.
  • Lid seal: Snap-lock or silicone-sealed lids are genuinely leak-proof; basic snap lids can weep liquid in a bag.
  • Stackability: Uniform container shapes stack cleanly in a fridge — mismatched shapes waste shelf space fast.
