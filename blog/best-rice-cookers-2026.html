Best Rice Cookers of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 8, 2026 8 min read

A good rice cooker does more than boil water. Modern models use fuzzy logic to adjust time and temperature based on the grain, delivering fluffy, perfectly cooked rice without babysitting the stove.

Whether you cook daily or just on weekends, investing in a quality rice cooker frees up a burner and virtually eliminates burned or mushy rice. These are the models worth buying in 2026.

What to Look For

  • Capacity: Choose by household size — 3-cup for 1–2 people, 5.5-cup for families, 10-cup for large batches.
  • Fuzzy Logic vs. Basic: Fuzzy logic models adjust heat on the fly; basic on/off cookers work fine for white rice but struggle with brown or mixed grains.
  • Keep-Warm Function: Look for extended keep-warm (12–24 hrs) that holds rice without drying it out.
  • Induction vs. Conventional Heating: IH models heat from all sides for more even cooking — worth the premium for rice enthusiasts.
  • Easy Cleaning: Removable, non-stick inner pots and dishwasher-safe lids save significant cleanup time.
