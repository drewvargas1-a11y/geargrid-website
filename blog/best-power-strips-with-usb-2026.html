Best Power Strips with USB in 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 4, 2026 8 min read

A power strip with USB ports eliminates wall-wart charger clutter — every USB port on the strip is a charging slot that doesn't block an AC outlet. In 2026 the best models include USB-C ports with Power Delivery (fast charging up to 65W for laptops and phones), USB-A ports for older devices, and surge protection rated in joules that actually protects connected electronics from voltage spikes.

We evaluated surge protection joule rating, USB charging speed, outlet count, cord length, and build quality across these four picks.

What to Look For

  • Surge protection rating: Rated in joules — higher is better. 500 joules is minimal protection. 1000 joules handles typical household surges. 2000+ joules handles major surges from nearby lightning strikes. Never use a power bar with 0 joule "surge protection" claim — it offers none.
  • USB-C Power Delivery: USB-C PD ports charge compatible phones at 18–65W — significantly faster than standard 5W USB-A charging. 65W PD charges most laptops. Check if the power strip has dedicated USB-C PD ports (not just shared USB-C ports).
  • Flat plug design: Flat plug power strips plug flush against the wall and fit behind furniture and under desks — standard angled plugs stick out 3+ inches from the wall.
  • Cord length: 3 feet reaches nearby outlets. 6 feet reaches across desks. 10 feet reaches from wall outlets to the center of a room. Never extend power strips with extension cords — buy a longer cord power strip instead.
