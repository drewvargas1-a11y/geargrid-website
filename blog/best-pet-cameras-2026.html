Best Pet Cameras in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 10, 2026 9 min read

Pet cameras let you check in on your animals when you are away, talk to them through two-way audio, and in some models toss treats remotely. The best ones in 2026 go well beyond basic surveillance — they track activity levels, send bark and meow alerts, and stream full 1080p video that actually shows you what your pet is doing.

We evaluated video quality, two-way audio, treat dispensing, and app reliability to find the best pet cameras.

What to Look For

  • 360-degree rotation: Motorized pan/tilt lets you follow your pet around the room rather than having a fixed viewing angle.
  • Night vision: Essential for checking on pets in a dark bedroom or crate area.
  • Treat dispenser: Adds interactive enrichment for dogs and cats. Works better for food-motivated animals.
  • Bark/meow detection: Smart audio alerts notify you of activity without streaming 24/7. Saves battery and data.
  • Subscription: Check if the useful features require a monthly plan. Some cameras lock cloud storage and alerts behind a paywall.
