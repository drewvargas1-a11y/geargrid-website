Best Gifts Under $25 of 2026

By GearGrid Staff November 10, 2026 3 min read

A small budget doesn't have to mean a throwaway gift. The best inexpensive gifts are practical tools people reach for all winter: something to light the way, warm cold hands, or make the house a little smarter.

These four picks are typically priced under $25, though prices change during holiday sales, so check the current price before you buy.

What to Look For

  • Everyday usefulness: A tool that gets used weekly feels more generous than a novelty item.
  • Seasonal fit: Winter gifts like hand warmers and headlamps get used right away.
  • Universal appeal: Choose things that suit almost anyone, especially for coworkers and exchanges.
  • Quality brands: Established brands at small prices beat no-name gadgets.
  • Stocking-friendly: Small items double as stocking stuffers.
