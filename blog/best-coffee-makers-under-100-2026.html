Best Coffee Makers Under $100 in 2026

GearGrid Staff May 26, 2026 8 min read

You don't need to spend hundreds of dollars to get a great cup of coffee at home. The drip coffee maker market in 2026 is stacked with reliable, well-built machines that brew hot, flavorful coffee consistently — all for under $100. Whether you're after a no-fuss 12-cup drip machine, a single-serve pod brewer, or a manual French press for maximum flavor, there's a right answer at every price point.

We researched thousands of verified owner reviews and cross-referenced expert opinions from Consumer Reports, Wirecutter, and America's Test Kitchen to find the best coffee makers under $100. These five picks cover every brewing style and household size.

Our Top Picks

#1 — Ninja CE251 12-Cup: Best Overall

The Ninja CE251 is our top overall pick because it delivers the full package at around $60 — a large 12-cup capacity, programmable brew time, a mid-range brew temperature that extracts maximum flavor, and a permanent filter so you're not constantly buying paper filters. The insulated thermal carafe option keeps coffee hot for hours without the burnt taste you get from a hot plate.

The brew cycle is fast — a full 12 cups in around 8 minutes — and the adjustable brew strength lets you dial in whether you want a lighter morning cup or a bold afternoon brew. The intuitive LCD interface is easy to program even half-asleep. At $60, this is genuinely excellent value, and it's one of the most reviewed drip coffee makers on Amazon for a reason. If you want one machine that covers every scenario for a household, this is the one.

#2 — Hamilton Beach 12-Cup (46290): Best Budget

At just $20, the Hamilton Beach 46290 is the best budget coffee maker you can buy. It's simple by design — no programmable timer, no fancy settings — but it brews a solid, hot cup of coffee reliably every single morning. The 12-cup glass carafe and hot plate keep coffee warm, and a permanent filter means no ongoing supply cost. The water reservoir is easy to fill, and the whole machine wipes clean in seconds.

For a college dorm, a first apartment, an office breakroom, or anyone who just wants coffee without fuss, the Hamilton Beach 46290 is the answer. It has tens of thousands of five-star reviews because it does exactly what it promises. There's no reason to spend more if this covers your needs.

#3 — Keurig K-Classic: Best Single-Serve

If you live alone or in a household where everyone wants a different coffee, the Keurig K-Classic at around $90 is the best single-serve machine under $100. Choose your cup size (6, 8, or 10 oz), pop in a K-Cup pod, and you have fresh coffee in under a minute. No waiting for a full pot to brew, no wasted coffee, no carafe to wash. The 48-oz removable water reservoir holds enough for multiple brews before a refill.

Pod variety is where Keurig really wins — there are over 500 K-Cup varieties across every major coffee brand, tea, hot chocolate, and specialty drinks. It's the most convenient morning coffee experience you can buy, and the K-Classic has earned its reputation as the workhorse of the Keurig lineup. It's compatible with the reusable My K-Cup filter if you want to use your own ground coffee and skip the pod cost.

#4 — Hamilton Beach Hot & Iced (49620): Best for Iced Coffee

The Hamilton Beach 2-Way Brewer 49620 at around $40 is the only machine on this list that brews both hot and iced coffee. The iced coffee mode brews a concentrated shot directly over ice in a travel tumbler, producing a cold, undiluted iced coffee in about 90 seconds. The hot brew side handles a standard 12-cup carafe for regular morning coffee. Two machines in one for $40 is genuinely exceptional value.

If you make iced coffee regularly and currently pour leftover hot coffee over ice (which makes it watered down and weak), this machine fixes that problem immediately. The travel tumbler included makes it ideal for commuters. This is the recommendation for any household that consumes both hot and cold coffee.

#5 — Bodum Brazil French Press: Best Manual Brewer

The Bodum Brazil French Press at around $20 is the best choice for anyone who wants maximum flavor extraction with zero electricity. French press coffee is widely regarded as the best-tasting brewing method because the grounds steep directly in water at the right temperature, pulling out oils and compounds that paper filters strip out. The result is a rich, full-bodied cup that drip machines simply can't replicate.

The Bodum Brazil is the entry point to French press — durable borosilicate glass, a solid stainless plunger and filter, and a sturdy polypropylene handle. It's available in 8-cup, 4-cup, and 3-cup sizes. There's no machine to maintain, no pods, no filters to buy. For coffee purists, home baristas, or anyone who wants the best-tasting coffee on this list, the French press delivers it for the lowest price.
