Best Artificial Christmas Trees of 2026

By GearGrid Staff October 19, 2026 3 min read

A good pre-lit artificial tree saves hours of untangling lights every year and lasts many seasons. The differences come down to fullness, branch-tip count, light count, and how the branches attach.

We focused on 7.5-foot trees, the most common size for 8-foot ceilings, and compared full, slim, and flocked shapes so you can match the tree to your space.

What to Look For

  • Height and ceiling: A 7.5-foot tree leaves room for a topper under an 8-foot ceiling.
  • Shape: Full trees are the most traditional; slim and pencil trees fit corners and apartments.
  • Branch tips: More tips usually means a fuller look. PE tips look more realistic than all-PVC trees.
  • Lights: Check the light count, warm white vs. multicolor, and whether one bulb going out affects the rest.
  • Hinged branches: Hinged branches fold down into place, which makes setup much faster than hook-on branches.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do artificial Christmas trees last?

A well-stored tree often lasts many seasons. Lights are usually the first thing to fail, so check whether replacement bulbs or fuses are included.

Should I choose warm white or multicolor lights?

Warm white looks classic and works with most decor; multicolor is more playful. Some trees offer both with a remote.

How do I fluff an artificial tree?

Start at the bottom and work up, separating each tip in different directions. It takes time but makes a big difference.

How should I store an artificial tree?

Use a storage bag or the original box in a dry place. Avoid hot attics, which can damage lights and wiring.
