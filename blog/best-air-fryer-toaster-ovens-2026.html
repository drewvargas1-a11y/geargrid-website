Best Air Fryer Toaster Ovens in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 28, 2026 8 min read

An air fryer toaster oven combines a full-size countertop oven with air frying capability — replacing two appliances in one compact unit. In 2026 the best models handle baking, broiling, toasting, roasting, dehydrating, and air frying with minimal hot spots. They preheat faster than a full oven, use less energy, and produce better air fry results than basket-style air fryers for larger batch cooking.

We evaluated air frying performance, oven capacity, preheat time, cooking evenness, and value across these four picks for different kitchen sizes.

What to Look For

  • Capacity: Toaster oven size is measured in cubic inches or slots of toast. 0.6 cu ft handles 6 slices of toast and a 12-inch pizza; 0.8+ cu ft fits a 5 lb chicken or 12x12 baking sheet.
  • Air fry vs. convection: Air frying is convection on high — a powerful fan circulates hot air rapidly. Quality air fryer ovens hit 50+ CFM airflow for genuine crispy results. Weak convection fans produce soft, steamed results.
  • Preset accuracy: Look for smart presets that auto-adjust time and temperature based on food type — Breville's Smart Oven uses Element IQ to direct heat where it's needed most.
  • Cleaning: Non-stick interiors and dishwasher-safe racks make cleanup manageable. Crumb trays are essential — look for full-width slide-out designs that catch everything.
