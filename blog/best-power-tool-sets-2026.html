Best Power Tool Sets in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 20, 2026 9 min read

Buying a power tool combo kit gives you multiple tools, a shared battery platform, and batteries and chargers included — a significantly better value than buying tools individually. The best 2026 kits from DeWalt, Milwaukee, and Ryobi cover every common household and DIY task in one purchase.

We evaluated motor quality, kit contents, battery capacity, brand ecosystem depth, and total value to find the best power tool sets for home use.

What to Look For

  • Battery platform: The battery ecosystem is the most important long-term consideration. DeWalt FLEXVOLT and MAX, Milwaukee M18, and Ryobi ONE+ are the three most extensive cordless platforms — each with hundreds of compatible tools. Once invested in a platform, staying within it saves money on batteries.
  • Included tools: The most useful combo kits include a drill/driver, impact driver, circular saw, and reciprocating saw as a minimum. Higher-value kits add oscillating tools, jigsaws, and LED worklights.
  • Battery capacity (Ah): A 2.0Ah battery gives you roughly 30-45 minutes of drilling. A 4.0Ah battery doubles runtime. Kits with two 4.0Ah+ batteries are significantly more usable than kits with two 1.5Ah batteries.
  • Brushless vs. brushed motors: Brushless motors are more efficient, run cooler, last longer, and extract more runtime per battery charge. At current prices, nearly all kits in the $250+ range use brushless motors.
  • Carrying case quality: Molded hard cases organize tools securely and withstand job site conditions. Soft bag cases are lighter but offer less protection. Kits used regularly in different locations benefit from hard case storage.
