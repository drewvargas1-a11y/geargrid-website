Best Heated Socks of 2026

By GearGrid Staff October 19, 2026 3 min read

Cold feet end more winter outings than anything else. Heated socks put thin heating elements across the toes and sole and run them from a small rechargeable battery that clips to the cuff, so you can add warmth exactly where circulation is weakest.

They're most useful when you're standing or sitting still in the cold: hunting, ice fishing, watching a game, or riding a chairlift. We compared battery capacity, heat zones, sock material, and how practical each pair is to charge and wash.

What to Look For

  • Battery capacity: Larger packs (5,000mAh and up) run longer. Every listing quotes its longest runtime on the lowest setting, so expect a fraction of that on high.
  • Heat coverage: Elements that wrap the toes and run under the sole warm more of the foot than toe-only designs.
  • Sock material: Wool blends stay warm when damp and resist odor; synthetic blends dry faster.
  • Controls: A button on each battery is standard; app or remote control lets you adjust without reaching for your ankles.
  • Washability: Check that the batteries detach and the socks can be hand or machine washed on gentle.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do heated socks stay warm?

It depends on the battery and the setting. Packs around 2,000mAh typically last a few hours, while 5,000mAh packs can run much of a day on low. High heat drains any battery quickly.

Can you wash heated socks?

Most can be hand washed or machine washed on a gentle cycle once the batteries are removed. Always follow the care tag and let them dry fully before reconnecting.

Are heated socks safe?

They use low-voltage batteries and are designed for skin contact, but don't sleep in them or use them if you have reduced sensation in your feet without checking with a doctor first.

Do heated socks fit in ski boots?

Most do, but the battery pack sits at the cuff, so check that it clears the top of your boot. Thinner socks fit tight boots better.
