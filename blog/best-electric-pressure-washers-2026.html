Best Electric Pressure Washers in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 10, 2026 9 min read

An electric pressure washer turns a 4-hour scrubbing job into 30 minutes. Driveways, decks, siding, patio furniture, vehicles, and gutters all respond to a pressure washer in a way that a hose and brush simply cannot match. Electric models have closed the power gap on gas while eliminating fumes and requiring far less maintenance.

We evaluated pressure output, GPM flow rate, hose quality, portability, and reliability to find the best electric pressure washers.

What to Look For

  • PSI vs GPM: PSI (pressure) breaks up dirt. GPM (gallons per minute) rinses it away. You need both — a high PSI, low GPM machine leaves soap residue. Look for at least 1.2 GPM.
  • Nozzle tips: 0-degree (red) for stubborn stains, 15-degree (yellow) for hard surfaces, 25-degree (green) for general use, 40-degree (white) for delicate surfaces. Most sets include all four.
  • Hose length: 20 feet is the minimum practical length. 25-35 feet gives you more reach without moving the machine.
  • Total Stop System: Automatically shuts off the pump when the trigger is released, extending pump life significantly.
