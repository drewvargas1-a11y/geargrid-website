Best Electric Lawn Mowers in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 12, 2026 9 min read

Gas mowers are losing their last advantage. Modern battery mowers deliver gas-level torque, run 45-60 minutes per charge, and skip the oil changes, pull cords, carburetors, and exhaust entirely. You press a button, mow, and hang the battery on a charger.

We evaluated cutting performance in thick grass, real-world runtime, deck size, drive systems, and price to find the best electric mowers for every yard — including corded options that never need charging at all.

What to Look For

  • Battery voltage and runtime: 40V-56V platforms with 4-6Ah batteries cut 30-60 minutes per charge — enough for a quarter to half acre. Bigger yards need a second battery.
  • Deck width: 21-inch decks match gas mowers and finish faster. 14-20 inch decks weigh less and maneuver tighter spaces, at the cost of more passes.
  • Push vs self-propelled: Self-propelled drive matters on slopes and thick lawns; on small flat yards it just drains battery. Variable-speed drive that matches your pace is worth having.
  • Corded option: Corded mowers cost less, weigh less, and never run out of power — if you can live with managing an extension cord on a smaller yard.
  • Brushless motors: Brushless motors run cooler, quieter, and longer than brushed. Every mower worth buying in 2026 uses one.
