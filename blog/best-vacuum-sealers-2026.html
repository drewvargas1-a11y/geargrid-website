Best Vacuum Sealers in 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 6, 2026 8 min read

A vacuum sealer extends refrigerator food life from 3–5 days to 2–3 weeks and freezer life from 6 months to 2–3 years by removing the oxygen that causes oxidation and freezer burn. In 2026 vacuum sealers are also the default accessory for sous vide cooking, bulk-buying at warehouse stores, and building emergency food storage. The best models seal dry food, liquid-rich marinades, and produce with the same reliability.

We evaluated vacuum strength, seal quality, bag compatibility, speed, and value across these four picks.

What to Look For

  • External vs. chamber vacuum sealers: External sealers (all consumer models) remove air from bags by pulling it through the bag edge. Chamber sealers evacuate a sealed chamber — better for liquid-rich foods. Consumer: external. Commercial: chamber.
  • Wet vs. dry food modes: Wet mode uses a lower vacuum speed to pull less liquid during sealing — important for soups, marinades, and fresh fruits. Without a wet mode, liquid gets drawn into the sealing area and compromises the seal.
  • Seal quality: Double seal creates two heat welds across the bag edge — redundancy reduces seal failures. Single seal is adequate for immediate refrigerator storage; double seal is better for long-term freezer storage.
  • Bag compatibility: Brand-specific bags are more expensive. Universal bags work with most sealers and cost less. Verify your sealer accepts universal bags — some FoodSaver models require proprietary bags.
