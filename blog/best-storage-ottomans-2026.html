Best Storage Ottomans in 2026: Hidden Storage, Real Style

By GearGrid Staff July 21, 2026 8 min read

A storage ottoman solves three problems at once: it functions as a footrest, provides extra seating when needed, and hides clutter inside a piece of furniture that looks like it belongs in the room. The best ones are sturdy enough to sit on (not all are), easy to open with one hand, and hold a meaningful amount of storage — not just a few throw pillows.

Here are the best storage ottomans of 2026, ranked by weight capacity (some collapse under adult weight), interior storage volume, lid mechanism, and style options.

What to Look For

  • Weight capacity: The most important spec for anyone who'll sit on the ottoman. Some decorative ottomans hold only 100 lbs — not suitable for adult seating. Look for 300+ lbs for reliable seating use. Always check the manufacturer's stated weight limit.
  • Lid mechanism: Lift-off lids provide the most interior access but require somewhere to set the lid. Hinged lids are more convenient but limit access angle. Reversible lids (tray on one side, cushion on the other) add versatility.
  • Interior volume: Measure what you plan to store — a standard throw blanket takes about 1.5 cubic feet unfolded. Most medium ottomans hold 1.5–3 cubic feet. Large ottomans (22"+ x 22"+) hold 3–5 cubic feet.
  • Fabric type: Faux leather wipes clean — good for families with kids and pets. Velvet looks premium but stains easily. Performance fabric (polyester blend) is the best all-around for households with heavy use.
  • Multi-function: Ottomans with trays convert to coffee tables. Sectional ottoman sets break apart into individual poufs. Bench-style ottomans work at the foot of a bed.
