Best Cordless Impact Drivers in 2026: Maximum Torque Ranked

By GearGrid Staff July 19, 2026 8 min read

An impact driver delivers 3–5x the torque of a standard drill by combining rotational force with rapid concussive impacts — making it the right tool for driving long screws, lag bolts, and fasteners through hard wood without cam-out or wrist strain.

Here are the best cordless impact drivers of 2026 selected for torque output, battery ecosystem, build quality, and value — from professional-grade to DIY-friendly budget options.

What to Look For

  • Impact driver vs. drill: Impact drivers are optimized for driving fasteners. Drills are optimized for drilling holes. Professionals own both. For DIY, an impact driver handles 80% of fastener tasks and many drill bits fit with a hex adapter.
  • Torque (in-lbs): 1800+ in-lbs handles most residential construction. 2000+ in-lbs is pro-grade for lag bolts and heavy timber framing. Home DIY tasks (furniture, decks) work fine at 1500+ in-lbs.
  • Battery voltage and system: 18V/20V platforms dominate. Buy into the same brand as your other cordless tools — batteries and chargers are interchangeable within a brand's platform.
  • Speed settings: Variable speed triggers with 3+ modes let you use lower speed for delicate materials without spinning out screws or stripping heads. Single-speed models are less forgiving.
  • Brushless vs. brushed: Brushless motors run cooler, last longer, and deliver 20–30% more run time. Worth the extra $20–30 at this price range.
