Best Portable DVD Players of 2026

By GearGrid Staff October 19, 2026 3 min read

Portable DVD players are still a road-trip favorite: no Wi-Fi, no data plan, and no streaming apps. Kids can watch their own discs, and many players also play movies from USB drives and SD cards.

We compared screen size, battery life, headrest mounting, and connectivity for car trips and holiday travel.

What to Look For

  • Screen size: 9 to 10.5 inches is a good balance for backseat viewing.
  • Battery life: Look for several hours of playback if you'll use it away from the car.
  • Headrest mount: A mount keeps the screen at eye level and out of little hands.
  • Media support: USB and SD card slots let you play digital files, not just discs.
  • Headphone jack: Lets kids watch without the whole car listening along.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do portable DVD players play Blu-ray?

Most do not. Check the listing for supported formats.

Are portable DVD players region-free?

Many advertise region-free playback, but confirm in the listing if you have discs from other countries.

Can I connect a portable DVD player to a TV?

Many have AV or HDMI output. Check the ports before you buy.

How long do batteries last?

Usually a few hours per charge. Plug into the car charger on long drives.
