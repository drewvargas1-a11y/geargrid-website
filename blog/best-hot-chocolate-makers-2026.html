Best Hot Chocolate Makers of 2026

By GearGrid Staff November 10, 2026 3 min read

A hot chocolate maker heats and whisks milk with chocolate or cocoa at the same time, giving you a smooth, frothy drink without scorched milk or lumps.

Many double as milk frothers for lattes, which makes them useful year-round. We compared capacity, heat control, and cleanup.

What to Look For

  • Capacity: Larger pitchers make several mugs at once for family movie nights.
  • Heat and mix modes: Separate modes let you heat cocoa or froth cold milk.
  • Detachable pitcher: Makes pouring and cleaning easier.
  • Cleaning: Check whether the pitcher is dishwasher safe or hand wash only.
  • Chocolate type: Some makers handle chocolate chips or shavings; others work best with cocoa powder.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you use chocolate chips in a hot chocolate maker?

Many makers handle chopped chocolate or chips; check the manual for recommended amounts.

Is a hot chocolate maker the same as a milk frother?

Many overlap. Hot chocolate makers usually have larger capacity and stronger mixing.

Can you make cold foam?

Models with a cold or mix-only mode can.

How do you clean a hot chocolate maker?

Rinse right after use. Some pitchers are dishwasher safe; others are hand wash only.
