Best Kids Smartwatches of 2026

By GearGrid Staff October 19, 2026 3 min read

A kids' smartwatch gives children a way to call and message approved contacts and lets parents see where they are, without handing over a full smartphone. Most require a cellular plan.

We compared calling, GPS, parental controls, battery life, and subscription requirements to help you choose the right first device.

What to Look For

  • Cellular plan: Most kids' watches need a monthly plan. Check the carrier and cost before buying.
  • Approved contacts: Parents should control exactly who can call and message the watch.
  • GPS and alerts: Location tracking, safe zones, and SOS buttons are the main safety features.
  • No internet or apps: Many parents prefer watches with no web browser or open app store.
  • Battery and durability: Look for all-day battery and water resistance for active kids.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do kids smartwatches need a phone plan?

Most do. Garmin, Gabb, and TickTalk each have their own plan or subscription requirements; check before you buy.

What age is right for a kids smartwatch?

Many families start around ages 5 to 7 as kids begin spending time away from parents.

Can kids browse the internet on these watches?

Gabb has no internet or apps. Others limit features through parental controls.

Are kids smartwatches allowed in school?

Rules vary by school. Many watches have a school mode that limits features during class.
