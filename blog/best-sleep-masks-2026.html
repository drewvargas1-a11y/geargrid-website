Best Sleep Masks of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 12, 2026 8 min read

A quality sleep mask blocks light completely and signals to your brain that it's time to sleep — making it one of the cheapest and most effective tools for better sleep at home, in hotel rooms, or on long-haul flights.

The best sleep masks in 2026 pair total blackout performance with comfort features that don't press on your eyes or leave strap marks on your face.

What to Look For

  • Contoured vs. Flat: Contoured masks cup around the eyes without pressing on the eyelids — allowing natural eye movement during REM sleep. Flat masks can press on eyes and cause discomfort.
  • Material: Silk is cool, soft, and gentle on skin. Memory foam molds to your face shape. Cotton is breathable and washable. Avoid masks with scratchy stitching around the eye area.
  • Light Blocking: True blackout means zero light leakage around the nose bridge. Test by putting the mask on before opening your eyes — any light is a fail.
  • Strap Comfort: Elastic straps dig in during long wear. Adjustable straps with soft material or velcro ends prevent pressure marks and fit every head size.
  • Weight: Lighter masks work better for side sleepers and travel. Heavy masks feel uncomfortable pressed against a plane seat or pillow.
