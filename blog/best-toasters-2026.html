Best Toasters of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 13, 2026 8 min read

A great toaster produces consistently even, golden-brown toast without burning the edges or leaving pale centers — and it does this the same way every single morning without babysitting. Bad toasters are surprisingly common.

These are the best toasters of 2026, from reliable 2-slice models for daily use to wide-slot 4-slice toasters that handle bagels, English muffins, and thick artisan bread.

What to Look For

  • Slot Width: Standard slots (1.2") handle regular sliced bread. Wide slots (1.5"–2") accommodate bagels, thick-cut bread, Texas toast, and artisan loaves. Wide slots are strongly preferred.
  • Browning Control: At minimum 5-6 browning levels. The difference between setting 3 and 4 should be visibly distinct and repeatable. Inconsistent shade control is the most common consumer complaint.
  • Extra Functions: Defrost mode thaws frozen bread before toasting. Bagel mode heats only the cut side. Cancel/lift button safely stops toasting mid-cycle.
  • Slot Count: 2-slice for individuals and couples. 4-slice for families or regular entertaining. Some 2-slot toasters have wider slots that fit two slices each — checking the footprint vs. utility tradeoff.
  • Crumb Tray: Removable, slide-out crumb trays that actually catch crumbs (not just gesture at it) make cleanup dramatically easier.
