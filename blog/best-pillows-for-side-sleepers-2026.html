Best Pillows for Side Sleepers of 2026

By GearGrid Staff July 8, 2026 8 min read

Side sleeping is the most common sleep position — and also the most demanding on pillows. You need a pillow that fills the gap between your ear and the mattress to keep your spine in neutral alignment. Too flat and your neck bends down; too thick and it bends up.

These are the best pillows for side sleepers in 2026, chosen for proper loft, durability, and the ability to stay supportive through the night.

What to Look For

  • Loft (Height): Side sleepers generally need a medium-to-high loft (4–6 inches) to fill the shoulder gap. Match loft to your shoulder width and mattress firmness.
  • Fill Material: Shredded memory foam is adjustable, supportive, and breathable. Solid foam offers firm support. Down and down-alternative are soft but may compress over time.
  • Adjustability: Adjustable fill pillows let you add or remove material to dial in the exact loft for your body — the best option if unsure where to start.
  • Cooling Features: Side sleepers press their face into the pillow. Gel-infused foam, bamboo covers, and open-cell foam all reduce heat retention.
  • Washability: A machine-washable cover is essential for hygiene. Some shredded foam pillows can be fully machine washed — check the tag.
