Best Smart Door Locks in 2026

By GearGrid Staff June 20, 2026 9 min read

A smart door lock eliminates fumbling for keys, lets you grant temporary access to guests and service providers without physical keys, and logs every entry with a timestamp. The best 2026 models integrate with your smart home platform and never require you to worry about being locked out.

We evaluated security ratings, keypad reliability, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi connectivity, battery life, and installation complexity to find the best smart door locks.

What to Look For

  • Security grade: ANSI Grade 1 is the highest residential security rating — the lock can withstand 250,000 cycles, a 6,250-lb dead-bolt shear force, and 75 door strikes. Grade 2 withstands 150,000 cycles. Always choose Grade 1 for exterior doors.
  • Entry methods: The best smart locks support keypad code entry, smartphone app, key fob, Apple HomeKit, and physical key as a backup. More entry methods mean fewer lockout scenarios.
  • Connectivity: Bluetooth-only locks require your phone to be nearby. Wi-Fi or Z-Wave enabled locks allow remote locking, unlocking, and monitoring from anywhere. A hub or bridge extends Bluetooth locks to Wi-Fi capability.
  • Battery life: Most smart locks run on AA batteries lasting 6-12 months. Auto-lock and high-frequency Bluetooth scanning drain batteries faster. Low battery alerts via app are essential.
  • Installation complexity: Most smart locks replace the interior portion of your existing deadbolt — installation takes 15-30 minutes with a screwdriver. Some require professional installation or a specific existing deadbolt model to be compatible.
