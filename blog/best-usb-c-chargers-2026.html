Best USB-C Chargers in 2026: GaN Fast Charging Ranked

By GearGrid Staff July 19, 2026 8 min read

GaN (gallium nitride) technology has completely changed the charger market. A GaN charger delivers the same power as older silicon chargers at half the size and a fraction of the heat — and a single 65W brick can now replace your phone charger, laptop charger, and tablet charger simultaneously.

Here are the best USB-C chargers of 2026, selected for wattage output, port configuration, safety certifications, and real-world charging speed across a range of devices.

What to Look For

  • Wattage: Phones need 18–45W max. MacBooks need at least 30W (61W+ for 16-inch). Most Windows laptops charge fine at 45–65W. A 100W+ charger covers everything.
  • GaN vs. Silicon: GaN chargers run cooler, charge faster, and are significantly more compact. At this point there's little reason to buy a silicon charger over GaN at the same price.
  • Port count: Multi-port chargers share wattage across ports — a 100W 4-port charger may drop to 45W per port when all four are active. Check the spec sheet.
  • Safety certs: Look for UL Listed, CE, and FCC certifications. Cheap off-brand chargers skip these and can damage batteries or cause fires.
  • Cable included: Most chargers don't include a USB-C cable. Buy a USB-C to USB-C cable rated for your wattage separately — not all cables support 100W.
