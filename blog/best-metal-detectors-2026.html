Best Metal Detectors of 2026

By GearGrid Staff September 9, 2026 8 min read

Metal detecting is a genuinely rewarding outdoor hobby — and the gear has gotten significantly better in the past five years. Modern mid-range detectors can discriminate between trash and targets, display target IDs on a screen, and handle most terrain types without expert knowledge.

What to Look For

  • VLF vs. PI Technology: VLF (very low frequency) detectors are best for coin and relic hunting in mineralized soil. PI (pulse induction) detectors penetrate highly mineralized soil and saltwater beaches better but can't discriminate targets as well. VLF is right for most hobbyists.
  • Discrimination: The ability to reject iron and foil while accepting coins and jewelry is the most practically useful feature. Multi-tone audio and visual target ID screens help identify what you've found before digging.
  • Operating Frequency: Lower frequency (5–8 kHz) detects large deep targets and coins. Higher frequency (15–40 kHz) is more sensitive to small gold nuggets and fine jewelry but misses deep large targets.
  • Waterproofing: Search coil waterproofing is standard. Full waterproofing (including the control box) is needed for water detecting. If you plan to hunt beaches or shallow water, look for fully waterproof models.
  • Weight and Balance: Metal detectors are held extended for hours — weight matters significantly. 2–3 lbs is the sweet spot. Heavier machines fatigue the arm and shoulder during long hunts.
