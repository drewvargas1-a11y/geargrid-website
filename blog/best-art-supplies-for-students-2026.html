Best Art Supplies for Students in 2026

By GearGrid Staff August 23, 2026 8 min read

Whether you're heading into an art class, architecture studio, or just love sketching on the side, having the right supplies makes everything easier. The difference between cheap construction paper and a quality heavyweight drawing sheet is immediately apparent — and can mean the difference between a project that impresses and one that bleeds, tears, or wrinkles at the worst moment.

We picked the four supplies that get the most consistent praise from students, teachers, and hobbyists: a heavyweight drawing paper trusted by professional artists, a colored pencil set that blends beautifully, a versatile sketch pad for daily use, and a watercolor set that punches well above its price. All are available on Amazon with fast shipping — perfect for back-to-school season.

What to Look For

  • Paper weight matters: For pencil, charcoal, and ink, look for at least 70 lb (105 gsm) paper. Lighter paper buckles under wet media. For watercolor, 90 lb (140 gsm) or higher is the minimum — cold-press texture grips paint better.
  • Archival quality saves your work: Acid-free, lignin-free paper won't yellow or become brittle over time. If you're making portfolio-worthy work, it's worth paying for archival-grade materials.
  • Pencil grades for drawing: H pencils (harder) make light, precise lines. B pencils (softer) make dark, rich marks. For sketching, a range from 2H to 6B gives you full tonal control. Most student sets cover 2H to 4B.
  • Colored pencil binders: Oil-based pencils (Prismacolor, Polychromos) blend smoothly and layer well. Wax-based pencils are softer but can leave bloom. For school work either is fine — oil-based lasts longer under heavy use.
  • Set size vs. quality: A 24-color set of quality pencils beats a 120-color set of low-quality ones. Start with a smaller, better set — you can mix and blend to create any color you need.
