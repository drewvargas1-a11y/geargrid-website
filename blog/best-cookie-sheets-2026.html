Best Cookie Sheets of 2026

By GearGrid Staff September 9, 2026 8 min read

A quality cookie sheet is the most fundamental baking tool in the kitchen. The difference between a warping, dark-bottomed cheap pan and a professional aluminum half-sheet is the difference between burned bottoms and perfectly even bakes.

What to Look For

  • Aluminum vs. Nonstick vs. Stainless: Bare aluminum heats the most evenly and produces the best baking results — it's what professional bakers use. Nonstick coatings add convenience but can scratch, brown bottoms faster, and don't handle high heat (450°F+) well. Stainless is durable but heats less evenly than aluminum.
  • Thickness/Gauge: Thicker aluminum (18-gauge) resists warping at high oven temperatures. Thin pans warp and create hot spots. Look for 'heavy gauge' or 'commercial grade' labels.
  • Rimmed vs. Rimless: Rimmed sheet pans (with raised edges) are more versatile — they work for cookies, roasting vegetables, sheet pan dinners, and bar cookies. Rimless are only useful for cookies.
  • Size: Half-sheet (18x13 inches) is the standard size for home ovens. Quarter-sheet (13x9) is useful for toaster ovens and smaller tasks. Check your oven's interior dimensions before buying a half-sheet.
  • Color: Light-colored pans reflect heat and bake more evenly. Dark pans absorb heat faster and brown bottoms more aggressively — useful for crispier results, risky for delicate cookies.
