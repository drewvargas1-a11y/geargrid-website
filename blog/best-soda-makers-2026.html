Best Soda Makers in 2026: Sparkling Water On Demand

By GearGrid Staff July 23, 2026 8 min read

A soda maker pays for itself in 3–6 months for anyone who drinks more than 2 liters of sparkling water per week. The math is simple: store-bought sparkling water runs $0.50–1.00/liter; a soda maker produces sparkling water for $0.08–0.15/liter once you account for CO2 refill costs. The environmental case (no plastic bottles) is equally compelling.

Here are the best soda makers of 2026 — from SodaStream's established ecosystem to newer machines with larger CO2 cylinders and stronger carbonation — ranked by carbonation strength, CO2 cost per liter, and ease of use.

What to Look For

  • CO2 cylinder size: Standard SodaStream cylinders make 60L per fill. Larger cylinders (130L, 150L) cost the same to refill but last twice as long — far more convenient. Verify your machine is compatible before upgrading cylinder size.
  • Carbonation strength: Manual machines let you control carbonation by holding the button longer — more pushes = more bubbles. Automatic machines have presets (low/medium/high). Manual gives more control but requires practice to get consistent results.
  • CO2 refill ecosystem: SodaStream cylinders can be exchanged at major retailers (Target, Walmart, Amazon, Bed Bath Beyond). Third-party cylinders (DrinkMate, Aarke) often require mail-in refill or specific retailers. Weigh convenience before committing to an ecosystem.
  • Bottle material: BPA-free plastic bottles are standard — replace every 3 years per manufacturer. Glass bottles exist for some models (Aarke, SodaStream Art) and are better for keeping carbonation long-term.
  • Noise level: Manual carbonation produces a burping/release sound when you vent excess CO2 — normal and unavoidable. Automatic machines are quieter. Neither is loud enough to be disruptive.
